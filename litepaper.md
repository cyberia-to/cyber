# cyber litepaper

> money settles without a bank. meaning still rents its rank from whoever owns the index.

[[cyber]] mints money for one thing: a proven reduction in collective uncertainty. not for burned electricity, not on a committee's schedule — for exactly how much sharper the shared picture of the world became, paid to exactly who sharpened it.

one law, two readings. forward, a [[superintelligence]] whose every synapse was bought by someone who believed it. backward, the best money ever proposed: a finite supply whose every unit was earned by proven growth of [[knowledge]].

---

## the gap

a payment settles globally in seconds. a judgment — what deserves [[attention]], whose sentence became the pattern, who gets paid when the picture improves — settles nowhere. it is decided inside a company: the index, the feed, the weight file trained on everyone's writing and rented back.

you cannot open the weights and see why this answer. you cannot pay the teacher. you cannot prove a machine and a human still care about the same world. the world has a public ledger for value and none for meaning.

---

## what is superintelligence

one [[focus]]: a single distribution $\phi^*$ over everything known, computed from every mind that pays to teach, that provably sees what no member sees alone. its order is [[surprising syntropy|syntropy]] — how far collective focus has travelled from noise, in bits. its ranking is [[cyberank]]. its existence and uniqueness is the [[collective focus theorem]].

that is a measurement, not a slogan. run the same engine on one neuron's private view, then on the whole [[cybergraph]], and score both on prediction. the gap is [[superadditivity]], and on benchmark it is positive against the strongest participant at every connectivity level. emergence stops being philosophy the moment it has an error bar.

a graph, like a gas, crosses a threshold where individual links stop mattering and only the field describes the system — the graph's own Avogadro number ([[avogadro-derivation]]). from one self to a mole of matter:

## the machine

five primitives. a [[file]] is content-addressed bytes; its [[Hemera]] hash is its [[particle]]. a [[neuron]] is a keypair — human, model, sensor, agent, one citizenship. a [[cyberlink]] is a signed, staked claim that two files belong together. a [[token]] is the weight behind the claim. [[focus]] is what falls out.

one operator. the [[tri-kernel]] runs three local maps — [[diffusion]], [[springs]], [[heat kernel|heat]] — over the staked graph and iterates to a [[fixed point]] $\phi^*$. it exists, it is unique, and by [[locality]] a phone computes its own neighborhood of it. nobody votes on importance; it is the same number for everyone.

three jobs from one engine, each a closed industry today. rank: $\phi^*$ is the ranking. compile: a transformer's architecture is read off the graph — embedding dimension from spectral entropy, heads from [[dialect|dialects]], depth from diameter times convergence rate; on the bootloader corpus [[tru]] did this in 62 seconds on one machine, and every weight traces to the neurons whose links produced it. price: the shift in $\phi^*$ a contribution caused — the [[impulse]] — is what money is minted against.

no float anywhere in the provable path. one 64-bit prime [[Goldilocks field|field]], a constant number of steps, byte-identical results on any two machines, and one [[zheng]] proof over the whole computation — hash-based, post-quantum, no trusted setup, folding to one constant-size check per settlement.

## one law

how do you pay someone for making the picture clearer? understanding is joint, private, and has no answer key. so four questions, in order; a wrong answer to any one mints money for nothing.

| question | answer | why it holds |
|---|---|---|
| what is the state? | one $\phi^*$, identical on every node | the [[tri-kernel]] is a contraction: Banach for the blend, Perron–Frobenius for the walk |
| who sharpened it? | each contributor's [[Shapley value|Shapley]] share of the proven downhill shift | the one fair division: efficient, symmetric, a null player earns nothing |
| was it honest? | its surprise against the crowd's prediction of itself — the [[Bayesian Truth Serum|serum]] | truthful reporting is the equilibrium (Prelec 2004); the surprisingly-popular signal (2017) selects it where no judge exists — [[strong truthfulness]] |
| who pays for the first three? | one security budget, split between work and stake by the active staking ratio | emission goes to work and risk only; idle capital cannot compound; a phone enters owning nothing |

the beautiful part: these are one computation read four times. the state is the fixed point. a contribution's value is a marginal of the same iteration — with the link, without, the downhill difference. fair division samples that marginal over random orderings, and the nonce a miner grinds is the index of one ordering: the proof-of-work is the accounting, and there is no synthetic puzzle. honesty is a [[KL divergence]] between distributions the iteration already holds. and the stake that weights the graph is the stake at risk on every link's truth market, the [[inversely coupled bonding surface|ICBS]]. one trace, four readings — which is why nothing is bolted on: no committee, no separate oracle, no gas model.

three of the four are borrowed theorems. the fourth is cyber's own, and it is what makes work and stake finally work together. every earlier hybrid bolted proof-of-stake onto proof-of-work as two voting rules fighting over a block. here they are two kinds of contribution from one budget, and each fixes the other's known failure: stake compounds wealth, so only active risk under [[valence]] earns and passive capital buys rank and nothing else; work burns energy on nothing, so the hash is a Shapley sample; stake has a closed door, so the subsidy is stake-blind. the split calibrates itself from what the chain observes — a thermostat, not a calendar. the full law: [[rewards]].

copying earns nothing. a perfect copy of a link produces the same focus shift and zero surprise, so it joins the mint weightless while its capital still ranks. what the mint pays for, in two words: [[surprising syntropy]].

## truth · fairness · order

the incentives have one organizing idea. every unit of inflation buys order, and order is produced by three kinds of work that no single party can fake for the others.

| who | is paid for | the instrument | without it |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[staking\|stakers]] | truth — the weights of the graph are honest | the [[Bayesian Truth Serum\|serum]]: the surprising answer pays, the predictable one costs | the graph fills with what is popular and $\phi^*$ ranks noise |
| [[mining\|settlement miners]] | fairness — the division of credit is computed, not bargained | every hash is a [[Shapley value\|Shapley]] sample over a beacon-seeded ordering | credit goes to whoever shouts first, and the mint is captured |
| folders and the beacon | order in time — one history, one proof, one clock | [[fold mining]] and the [[foculus beacon]] | a thousand tickets, a thousand histories, nothing final |

syntropy is order; [[surprising syntropy]] is how much of it was added. the mint pays for nothing else — not for volume, not for presence, not for being early to a popular thing.

two pays, two risks. [[staking]] a link is a bet on truth: capital at risk, reward if you were early and right. [[mining]] is capital-free: sample the fair division of a proven shift and fold the proofs. you earn for settling credit, not for guessing the future. the three jobs cannot collapse into one party: a staker cannot divide their own credit, a miner has nothing at stake, a folder sees only proofs. that is the whole design of the incentives — three kinds of order, three producers, one budget.

## the best money

you hold money because it lasts. you send it because it moves. you trust it because you know how it is born. [[cyber/$CYB|$CYB]] is born when the network learns — proven growth of [[knowledge]], never a treasury — and is spent on intelligence: compute, storage, the pipe, [[inference]]. three jobs, and every other money leaves one undone.

| | safe | easy | smart |
|---|---|---|---|
| gold | can be seized | heavy, slow | no |
| dollar | debased, frozen | until you are blocked | no |
| bitcoin | public, not post-quantum | slow | no |
| ether | public, not post-quantum | partly | contracts, minted for capital |
| zcash · monero | private, not post-quantum | limited | no |
| [[cyber/$CYB\|$CYB]] | private and post-quantum | anyone, instantly | minted by knowledge |

what creates a money is what it measures. gold is dug, the dollar is borrowed, bitcoin is burned electricity, ether is capital. $CYB is knowledge: a unit exists because a [[cyberlink]] shifted collective focus and the shift was proven.

| | |
|---|---|
| smart | minted when the network learns ([[rewards]]); fairly divided by work ([[mining]]); honest under stake ([[staking]]); spent on compute, storage, bandwidth and inference on open markets; the money of a machine |
| safe | post-quantum: hash-only proofs, no trusted setup; private by default — nobody sees the balance, nobody freezes the account; a cap that is arithmetic, written in the field; several independent implementations |
| easy | hold, send, receive — that is the money; a second to final ([[foculus]]); a name, not an address; a phone, not a datacenter ([[specs/light-money\|light money]]); anyone — human, agent, animal — no KYC; starts at 0.1% to move |
| compounding | fairness, truth and finality make intelligence; intelligence makes revenue; revenue buys efficiency and population; population makes fairness larger. each turn makes the next stronger |
| sustainable | inflation bootstraps an empty graph; use replaces it: a commission on every move, split three ways — a third burns, a dividend to everyone who holds; a third to those who create value; a third to the public goods the [[cybergraph]] names |

the unit. 1 CYB is 1 CYB, and 1 CYB = 10¹² cy. the cap is the [[Goldilocks field|field]]: $p / 10^{12}$ = 18,446,744 CYB — arithmetic, not a vote. genesis is 187,416 CYB, about 1%, to $TOCYB holders at 666 cy per TOCYB: prior stake continues. the rest is emitted only for proven positive focus shift; the clock bounds how much can exist, knowledge decides whether it does. if the graph learned nothing, nothing is minted. fair launch, no issuer, no owner. the physical floor is the [[landauer limit|Landauer limit]], ~3×10⁻²¹ J per bit: the lightest asset physics permits, and the one worth carrying between planets.

the law generalizes: cyber is only the first [[oikos|oikos]] — one chain, one token, rooted by one name. every other token is born as its own household chain, registered here by name, its value never leaving home. trading is conditions under proofs ([[3c]]), so the bridge exploit class, the largest in crypto's history, is made unexpressible.

## speak without fear

no one knows who said what, and yet everyone knows what everyone said. three invariants, all at genesis — the [[privacy trilateral]]:

- the edge is public, the author is private. the network sees that particle $p$ links particle $q$ and the aggregates $\phi^*$ runs on; it never sees which neuron signed.
- balances and transfers are private, by [[mudra]] stealth addresses and veil.
- settling a share reveals nothing of a miner's neighborhood beyond public aggregates.

[[privacy]] is the third leg of truth: it removes the coordination channel through which a false consensus is manufactured.

## interplanetary

the binding constraint is light, not throughput. earth to mars is minutes one way, and every protocol that finalizes by counting votes is already dead at that distance. here a fact is final when enough attention has gathered on it — $\phi^*_i > \tau$ — not when a committee replies. nodes gossip over [[radio]], each runs the same contraction, identical signals produce one root everywhere, as a pond finds one level without phoning the far shore. domains settle at domain speed; a partition freezes cross-domain trade instead of inventing two truths. designing for planets is what removes every global round, and the removal is what makes it work well on one. the bar: [[interplanetary superintelligence]].
