1809-1882. British naturalist and geologist.

Established [[evolution]] by [[natural selection]] as the mechanism driving the diversity of life.

Published "On the Origin of Species" (1859), the foundation of modern [[biology]].

Demonstrated that all species descend from common ancestors through variation, inheritance, and selection.

His theory unifies [[biology]], [[genetics]], ecology, and paleontology under a single explanatory framework.

[[evolution]] by selection operates in any replicating system with variation and fitness pressure, including protocols and [[knowledge graph]] topologies.
