# neptune

The eighth planet from the [[sun]] and the outermost giant in the [[solar system]]. Neptune is an ice giant composed primarily of water, ammonia, and methane ices surrounding a rocky core.

Winds on Neptune exceed 2000 km/h, the fastest recorded in the solar system. The Great Dark Spot, observed by [[Voyager 2]] in 1989, demonstrated massive storm systems comparable to Jupiter's.

Neptune orbits at roughly 30 AU, taking 165 Earth years to complete one circuit. Its largest moon, [[Triton]], follows a retrograde orbit — evidence of gravitational capture from the [[Kuiper belt]].

The planet's deep blue color comes from methane in the upper atmosphere absorbing red wavelengths of sunlight.
