The unified [[field]] theory of electric and magnetic interactions, described by Maxwell's equations.

electric [[field]]: generated by charges, exerts [[force]] on other charges

magnetic [[field]]: generated by moving charges and changing electric fields

Maxwell's equations unify electricity, magnetism, and light into one framework

[[radiation]] is self-propagating oscillation of electromagnetic [[field]]

light is an electromagnetic [[wave]] traveling at the speed of light in [[spacetime]]

electromagnetic [[force]] is one of four fundamental forces — see [[force]]

basis for optics, electronics, and [[quantum mechanics]] via quantum electrodynamics

spectrum spans radio, microwave, infrared, visible light, ultraviolet, X-ray, gamma — see [[radiation]]
