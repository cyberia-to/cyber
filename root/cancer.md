uncontrolled cell division resulting from accumulated mutations in growth-regulating genes. cancer is the second leading cause of death globally and a primary target of phytochemical research

many [[species]] in the [[cyber valley]] catalog produce [[anticancer]] compounds. [[flavonoids]], [[terpenes]], [[alkaloids]], and [[phenolic compounds]] interfere with cancer cell proliferation through multiple mechanisms: apoptosis induction, angiogenesis inhibition, cell cycle arrest, and metastasis suppression

[[oxidative stress]] contributes to cancer initiation by damaging DNA. antioxidant compounds from [[fruit]], [[vegetables]], and [[herbs]] help neutralize reactive oxygen species, reducing mutation rates
