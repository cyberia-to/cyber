1856-1943, Serbian-American inventor and electrical engineer

developed alternating current (AC) induction motor and polyphase AC power system, the foundation of modern electricity distribution

pioneered wireless energy transmission experiments at Colorado Springs and [[Wardenclyffe Tower]]

invented the Tesla coil: resonant transformer circuit for high-voltage, low-current, high-frequency AC electricity

contributed to radio technology, rotating magnetic fields, and remote control

envisioned a global wireless communication and power network, prefiguring planetary-scale information systems

300+ patents across 26 countries in electrical and mechanical engineering

contemporary and rival of [[Thomas Edison]] in the AC vs DC current wars
