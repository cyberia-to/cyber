# coffee

A beverage brewed from roasted seeds of [[Coffea]] species, cultivated across tropical regions worldwide.

Contains [[caffeine]], a stimulant alkaloid that enhances alertness and cognitive function.

Major global commodity crop, with Arabica and Robusta as the two dominant cultivated [[species]].

Flavor profiles depend on origin, altitude, processing method, and roast level, shaped by [[terpenes]] and organic acids.

Brewing methods range from espresso and pour-over to cold extraction, each highlighting different aromatic compounds.

[[Coffee]] grounds enrich [[compost]] and serve as a natural soil amendment in [[permaculture]] systems.
