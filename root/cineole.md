a monoterpenoid found in many [[essential oil]]s, providing cooling and [[anti-inflammatory]] effects

cineole is the dominant compound in [[eucalyptus]] oil and occurs in [[rosemary]], [[tea tree]], [[bay laurel]], and [[sage]]

it exhibits [[antimicrobial]], [[antioxidant]], and bronchodilatory activity, making it useful in respiratory [[medicine]] and [[aromatherapy]]

cineole penetrates biological membranes readily, enhancing the delivery of other therapeutic compounds
