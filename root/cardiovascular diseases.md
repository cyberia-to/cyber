diseases of the heart and blood vessels — the leading cause of death worldwide. atherosclerosis (arterial plaque buildup), hypertension, and thrombosis are the primary pathologies

[[phytochemicals]] from [[plants]] address multiple cardiovascular risk factors. [[flavonoids]] improve endothelial function and reduce [[oxidative stress]]. [[oleic acid]] from [[olive oil]] lowers LDL cholesterol. [[phytosterols]] compete with cholesterol for intestinal absorption. [[phenolic compounds]] inhibit platelet aggregation

dietary patterns rich in [[fruit]], [[vegetables]], [[herbs]], and healthy fats consistently reduce cardiovascular mortality. the mechanisms converge on reducing inflammation, oxidation, and thrombotic tendency
