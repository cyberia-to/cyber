Tea is a [[beverage]] made by infusing [[leaves]] in hot [[water]].
The canonical tea plant, [[Camellia sinensis]], yields [[green tea]], [[black tea]], [[oolong]], [[white tea]], and [[pu-erh]] depending on [[oxidation]] and processing method.

Herbal infusions — [[chamomile]], [[mint]], [[rooibos]], [[ginger]] — are also called tea in everyday usage, though they contain no [[Camellia sinensis]] leaf.
The drink originated in [[China]] several thousand years ago and spread along [[trade]] routes to [[Japan]], [[India]], [[Europe]], and beyond.

Tea contains [[caffeine]], [[L-theanine]], and [[polyphenols]], a combination that promotes calm alertness and [[antioxidant]] activity.
Preparation variables — [[temperature]], steep time, leaf-to-water ratio — shape the flavor profile from delicate floral to robust malty.

In many cultures, tea ceremony and daily tea practice function as a form of [[meditation]] and social bonding.
[[Kombucha]], a fermented tea, introduces [[probiotics]] and bridges tea culture with [[fermentation]] traditions.
