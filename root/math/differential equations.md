Equations relating [[functions]] to their [[derivatives]], modeling how systems evolve over time and space.

[[ODE]] (ordinary differential equation):: involves derivatives with respect to a single variable

[[PDE]] (partial differential equation):: involves partial derivatives across multiple variables

[[Navier-Stokes equations]] govern fluid dynamics; [[Maxwell's equations]] govern electromagnetism

[[Schrodinger equation]] governs [[quantum mechanics]]; [[Einstein field equations]] govern [[relativity]]

[[existence and uniqueness theorems]] guarantee solutions under specified conditions

[[dynamical systems]] and [[chaos theory]] study long-term behavior and sensitivity to initial conditions

Solved via [[calculus]], [[linear algebra]], [[fourier transform]], and numerical methods
