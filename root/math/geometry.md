The study of shape, [[space]], position, and measurement.

[[Euclidean geometry]]:: flat space governed by five postulates, the classical framework

[[non-Euclidean geometry]]:: curved spaces (hyperbolic, elliptic) where the parallel postulate fails

[[differential geometry]]:: uses [[calculus]] on [[manifolds]], the language of [[relativity]]

[[Riemannian metric]] defines distance and curvature on smooth manifolds

[[symmetry]] and [[transformation]] groups connect geometry to [[algebra]]

[[projective geometry]] studies invariants under projection, central to computer vision

[[fractal]] geometry describes self-similar structures with non-integer dimension

Related:: [[topology]], [[linear algebra]], [[graph theory]], [[optimization]], [[quantum mechanics]]
