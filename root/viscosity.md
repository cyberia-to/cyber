resistance of a fluid to flow under applied [[force]]

measured in pascal-seconds (Pa-s) or poise

[[water]] has low viscosity (~0.001 Pa-s at 20 C), [[honey]] high (~2-10 Pa-s)

[[blood]] is intermediate and exhibits shear-thinning (non-Newtonian) behavior

decreases with rising [[temperature]] in liquids, increases in gases

dynamic viscosity vs kinematic viscosity (dynamic / [[density]])

governs lubrication, pipe flow, sedimentation, and magma movement

Reynolds number relates viscosity to flow regime: laminar vs turbulent

critical in industrial processes: coatings, food processing, petroleum extraction
