Musa, a tropical [[fruit]] [[plant]] of the [[Musaceae]] family providing a high-energy [[staple]] food

banana [[fruit]] is rich in [[potassium]], [[vitamin B6]], [[fiber]], and rapidly available carbohydrates, making it a primary energy source in tropical regions

cultivated varieties are parthenocarpic, producing seedless fruit through vegetative propagation, which limits genetic diversity and increases vulnerability to pathogens such as [[Fusarium]]

banana leaves serve as natural wrapping and cooking surfaces, while the [[flower]] and pseudostem are consumed as vegetables in South and Southeast Asian cuisines
