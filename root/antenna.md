device converting electric signals to electromagnetic (radio) waves and back

transmitting antenna: accelerates electrons, radiating energy into space

receiving antenna: electromagnetic waves induce current in conductor

types: dipole, monopole, parabolic dish, phased array, patch, loop, Yagi-Uda

parameters: gain, bandwidth, radiation pattern, polarization, impedance

parabolic dish focuses signals for [[telescope]] and satellite communication

phased array steers beam electronically using [[semiconductor]] control

communication infrastructure: radio, television, cellular, Wi-Fi, deep space

[[energy]] harvesting antennas (rectennas) convert ambient RF to DC power

meshes of antennas form the physical layer of [[cyberia]] network connectivity
