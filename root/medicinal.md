# medicinal

A plant property indicating therapeutic uses in traditional or evidence-based medicine.

[[Species]] with medicinal properties produce bioactive [[chemical compounds]] including [[alkaloids]], [[flavonoids]], [[terpenes]], and [[essential oils]].

Therapeutic applications range from anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial action to immune modulation and pain relief.

Ethnobotanical knowledge accumulated over millennia guides the identification of candidate species for pharmacological study.

Medicinal plants serve as the foundation of [[herbal]] medicine systems across every inhabited continent.

Proper identification, harvesting timing, and preparation methods determine the safety and efficacy of plant-derived remedies.
