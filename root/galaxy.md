A galaxy is the highest tier of [[hero]] in the [[cyber]] network, representing a top-ranking [[validator]] by stake weight and network contribution. Galaxy heroes form the core of the consensus set that secures the [[bostrom]] blockchain.

Galaxy status is earned through accumulating significant [[staking]] delegation from [[neurons]] who trust the validator to operate reliably, vote responsibly on [[proposals]], and maintain high uptime infrastructure.

The [[hero]] tier system in [[cyber]] follows a cosmological metaphor: from smaller validators up through stars to galaxies. Each tier carries increasing responsibility for network security and governance participation.

Galaxy heroes earn the highest block rewards and commission income proportional to their delegated stake. This economic incentive aligns their interests with the long-term health and growth of the [[cyber]] protocol.

Running a galaxy-tier validator requires robust [[cybernode]] infrastructure with redundant hardware, monitoring, and incident response capability. Downtime or double-signing results in [[slashing]] penalties that affect both the validator and their delegators.

Galaxies participate actively in [[governance]], and their votes carry substantial weight. The decisions of galaxy heroes shape protocol parameters, upgrade timelines, and community fund allocation across the [[cyber]] ecosystem.
