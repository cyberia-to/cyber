## The Builder's Creed
- We value agency over dependency
- In this community, we distinguish between two modes of participation:

## What we practice

- "I found someone" over "we need someone"
- "Here's an algorithm" over "someone should figure this out"
- "I'll build a prototype" over "we need a product"
- "I contacted X, here's what they said" over "we should reach out to people"*

## The Sovereignty Principle

- Before raising a need, ask yourself:
- > *"Am I proposing something within my control, or requesting something outside of it?"*
- Sovereign contribution = specific person + specific action + your commitment to facilitate
- Parasitic pattern = abstract category + vague expectation + zero personal stake

## Examples

| Parasitic | Sovereign |
|---|---|
| "We need VCs" | "I know Alex at [Fund], I'll intro us next week" |
| "We need marketing" | "I wrote a thread, here's the draft for feedback" |
| "We need a market maker" | "I analyzed 3 MMs, here's comparison and my recommendation" |

## The Rule

- Don't wish for resources
- Either bring them, build them, or design the path to them
