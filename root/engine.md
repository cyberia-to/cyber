device converting [[energy]] into mechanical work

types: internal combustion (gasoline, diesel), electric motor, steam, jet, rocket

internal combustion: fuel-air mixture ignites in cylinders, drives pistons and crankshaft

electric motor: electromagnetic interaction between stator and rotor, high efficiency

steam engine: heat boils [[water]], expanding steam drives piston or turbine

jet engine: continuous combustion, thrust from exhaust gas acceleration

efficiency measured as ratio of useful work to input [[energy]]

powers transport, [[pump]] systems, generators, industrial machinery

electrification of engines is the core transition of the 21st century energy system

[[battery]] and [[semiconductor]] controls enable modern electric drivetrains
