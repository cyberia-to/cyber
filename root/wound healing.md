the biological process of tissue repair after injury — a cascade of inflammation, proliferation, and remodeling. effective wound healing requires immune defense against infection, new tissue formation, and extracellular matrix reorganization

many [[species]] produce compounds that accelerate wound healing. [[flavonoids]] and [[terpenes]] reduce wound inflammation. [[antibacterial]] compounds prevent secondary infection. [[vitamin]] C is essential for collagen synthesis — the structural protein that rebuilds damaged tissue

[[honey]] applied topically provides an [[antibacterial]] moist environment that promotes healing. aloe vera, turmeric ([[curcumin]]), and [[rosemary]] extract are documented wound-healing agents across [[traditional medicine]] systems
