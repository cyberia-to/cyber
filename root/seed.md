embryonic plant enclosed in a protective coat, carrier of genetic information

contains embryo, endosperm (nutrient store), and seed coat (testa)

dormancy mechanisms: physical, chemical, thermal, light-dependent

germination triggered by [[water]], temperature, and sometimes fire or scarification

vehicle of heredity across [[species]] generations

seed saving: farmer practice preserving [[crops]] diversity for millennia

modern seed banks (Svalbard, Vavilov) store genetic resources against extinction

open-pollinated vs hybrid vs genetically modified lineages

foundation of [[agriculture]]: who controls the seed controls the food supply

seed sovereignty is a core demand of [[food sovereignty]]
