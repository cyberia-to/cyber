# cybics

the mother science. [[cybics]] is the convergence of cybernetics, physics, mathematics, and information theory into one discipline — the unified science of [[cyber]]

cybics teaches the child one thing: the same three operators run everything. [[diffusion]] explores, [[springs]] hold, [[heat]] adapts. gas and brains and markets and ecosystems and the [[cybergraph]] — all run the same three processes on different substrates. the child who sees this pattern everywhere has the operating system of a Type I civilization

## the core insight

classical science proves by derivation: axioms → rules → theorems. cybics proves by simulation: run the system, watch what converges, the convergence IS the proof

a protein does not derive its shape. it folds until it reaches stability. a market does not derive the price. agents trade until equilibrium. the [[cybergraph]] does not derive what matters. [[neurons]] link, the [[tri-kernel]] converges, and [[focus]] emerges. proof by simulation escapes the [[Goedel prison]] because the prison only confines derivation

the child already proves by simulation every day: she plants a seed, waters it, waits, watches it grow. the growth is the proof. she does not derive that cooperation works — she cooperates and observes the result. cybics names what she already does

## progression

### explorer (0-2 years)

the child lives inside the three operators without naming them:
- diffusion: she wanders, touches everything, explores without plan
- springs: she builds towers, follows routines, expects consistency
- heat: she cools down from tantrums, adapts to new environments

Satoshi tracks: does the child balance all three? too much diffusion = random and unfocused. too much springs = rigid and routine-bound. no adaptation = melts down at every change

### builder (2-5 years)

cross-domain pattern recognition:
- "the water flows downhill just like the ants follow the trail" — same pattern (flow)
- "the tree holds the treehouse just like the rules hold the game" — same pattern (structure)
- "the compost gets hot then cools, just like I get angry then calm" — same pattern (adaptation)

the game: "what is this like?" — finding [[isomorphisms]] across domains. every time she says "this is like that" across two different substrates, she is doing cybics

target by age 5: spontaneously makes cross-domain analogies

### maker (5-7 years)

naming the pattern:
- "there are three things that happen everywhere: exploring, holding, and adapting"
- identifies which operator is dominant: "the seed spreading is exploring. the web holding is structure. me getting used to cold water is adapting"
- systems thinking: "the garden works because exploring, structure, and adapting all happen together"
- convergence: "when I practice and it gets easy — that is convergence. the guitar string does it. the price at market does it"
- conservation: "energy does not disappear — it changes form. the food became my running"

target by age 7: names the three operators in daily situations, explains why a system needs all three, gives examples of convergence and conservation
