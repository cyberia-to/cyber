# construction

The process of building physical structures from raw and processed materials.

In the [[cyber valley]] context: earthbag, bamboo, and [[permaculture]]-integrated building methods define the approach.

Natural [[construction]] prioritizes locally sourced materials, thermal mass, and passive climate regulation.

Earthbag walls use filled polypropylene sacks stacked in courses, reinforced with barbed wire and plastered with earth or lime.

Bamboo framing provides high tensile strength at a fraction of the weight and cost of steel.

Living roofs, rainwater harvesting, and integrated [[food forests]] blur the boundary between structure and [[ecosystem]].
