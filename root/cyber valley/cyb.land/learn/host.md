host your educational event at cyb.land.

we open the platform to indonesian teachers, makers, healers, scientists, and storytellers — anyone with knowledge worth sharing.

## what we provide

- venue: lecture hall (50 ppl), open-air pavilion (100 ppl), banya circle (12 ppl), forest classrooms (20 ppl)
- infrastructure: [[cyberlink]] high-speed wifi, projector, sound, recording
- audience: visitors with [[daypass]] / [[nightpass]] tickets, monthly [[nomads]], local community
- on-site logistics: [[organiq]] catering, [[ride]] shuttles, [[parking]]
- payment processing: card, crypto, on-arrival cash via the cyb.land cart
- promotion: featured on cyb.land, social channels, on-site signage

## what you bring

- a clear topic and audience-fit
- your own teaching materials and curriculum
- a date or recurring schedule
- (optional) co-hosts, guests, supporters

## revenue split

| model | host | cyb.land |
|---|---|---|
| ticketed event | 70% | 30% |
| sponsored event (covered by partner) | 100% | venue fee only |
| free / community event | n/a | venue at cost |

venue fees and revenue share are negotiated per event.
