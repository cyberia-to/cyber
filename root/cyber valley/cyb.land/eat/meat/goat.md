pasture-raised goat — milk-fed, slaughtered on site by trained handlers.

served three ways at the evening dose:

- boiled — slow-cooked tender, with broth (see [[goat broth]] for the broth-only version)
- grilled — over wood fire with rosemary and lime
- roasted — rubbed with garam masala, slow-roasted, served with rice
