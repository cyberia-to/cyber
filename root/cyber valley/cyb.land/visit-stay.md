# visit & stay

plan your visit to [[cyber valley]]. tell us when, who, and how — get a curated list of bookable options.

## inputs

- dates of arrival and departure
- group size and type (solo · couple · family · group)
- overnight wish (day visit · glamping night · private camp)
- preferences (banya, meal doses, vehicle rentals, hikes)

## output

a personalised list of bookable items matching your inputs — with prices, real-time availability, and direct booking links. add to cart, confirm, pay.
