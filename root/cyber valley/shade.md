# shade

A plant function providing canopy cover that reduces direct sunlight for understory [[species]].

Shade-providing [[tree]] species are essential in tropical [[permaculture]] and [[food forest]] design.

Canopy density, leaf size, and crown architecture determine the degree of light filtration reaching lower layers.

Strategic shade placement regulates soil moisture, reduces evapotranspiration, and creates microclimates for shade-tolerant crops.

In [[cyber valley]], shade trees anchor the upper canopy layer and support vertical diversity across the landscape.
