# local farmers

A source category in [[cyber valley]] recipes. Local farmers provide fresh produce, eggs, meat, and dairy within the [[cyber valley]] region.

Sourcing from local farmers reduces transport distance and ensures seasonal freshness. Direct relationships with growers build trust and traceability across the food chain.

Ingredients tagged as [[local farmers]] come from within the immediate growing region surrounding [[cyber valley]].

Pairing local sourcing with [[imported]] goods creates a complete ingredient palette for any recipe.
