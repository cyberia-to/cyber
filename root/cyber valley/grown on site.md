# grown on site

Source category in [[cyber valley]] recipes indicating ingredients cultivated directly on the land.

Gardens, [[food forest]] layers, and [[permaculture]] zones supply fruits, vegetables, herbs, and nuts.
On-site cultivation ensures freshness, eliminates transport costs, and closes nutrient loops.
Seasonal availability determines which ingredients carry this designation in any given recipe.
Crops [[grown on site]] connect the kitchen to the soil through traceable, hyperlocal supply chains.
This category distinguishes homegrown produce from foraged, traded, or purchased ingredients.
