# maintenance

Ongoing care tasks for cultivated [[plant]] species in [[cyber valley]].

Watering schedules adapt to seasonal rainfall patterns and soil moisture readings.
Pruning removes dead or competing branches to direct energy toward fruit and flower production.
Mulching with organic matter suppresses weeds, retains moisture, and feeds soil microbiota.
Pest management relies on companion planting, biological controls, and manual removal.
Soil amendment through composting, cover cropping, and mineral supplementation restores fertility each cycle.
Regular monitoring of [[growth]] stages informs the timing of each care intervention.
