SI unit of [[force]], equal to the force needed to accelerate 1 kg at 1 m/s2

1 N = 1 kg-m/s2

named after Isaac Newton, who formulated the laws of motion and universal gravitation

weight of a 1 kg mass on Earth: ~9.81 N

Newton's second law: F = m * a, the defining relation

connects to [[joule]] (1 J = 1 N-m), [[pascal]] (1 Pa = 1 N/m2), and [[watt]] (1 W = 1 N-m/s)

a typical apple weighs about 1 newton

forces in engineering range from micronewtons (MEMS devices) to meganewtons (rocket thrust)
