a food forming the dominant part of a [[diet]], consumed regularly and in sufficient quantity to constitute the bulk of energy and nutrient intake

staple foods are typically [[plant]]-based, including [[grain]]s such as [[rice]], [[wheat]], and [[maize]], [[tuber]]s such as [[potato]] and [[cassava]], and [[fruit]]s such as [[banana]] and [[plantain]]

the selection of staples varies by region, climate, and agricultural tradition, reflecting local [[soil]], [[water]], and cultural practices
