1815-1852. British mathematician and writer.

Wrote the first algorithm intended for machine execution, for [[Charles Babbage]]'s Analytical Engine.

Recognized that a general-purpose [[computation]] engine could manipulate symbols beyond pure arithmetic, foreseeing software.

Her notes on the Analytical Engine contain the clearest early articulation of what a program is.

Daughter of poet Lord Byron; combined mathematical rigor with imaginative vision.

The first person to distinguish between the machine (hardware) and the process (software), a separation central to every protocol including [[cyber]].
