system for organizing days into repeating cycles aligned to astronomical events

solar calendars: Gregorian (365.2425 days), Julian, Egyptian

lunar calendars: Islamic Hijri (354 days), Hebrew

lunisolar: Chinese, Hindu, Buddhist

the Gregorian calendar is the global civil standard since 1582

Unix epoch: seconds since 1970-01-01 00:00:00 UTC, the timekeeping standard of the [[Information Age]]

block height as time in [[cyber]]: each block is an irreversible tick of consensus time

calendars encode cultural, agricultural, and religious rhythms into shared temporal structure

precondition for coordinated [[agriculture]], trade, navigation, and [[science]]
