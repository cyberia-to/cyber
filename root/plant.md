A multicellular organism that converts light into chemical energy through photosynthesis. plants form the foundation of terrestrial ecosystems, providing food, medicine, building materials, and atmospheric oxygen

in [[cyber valley]], plants serve as both sustenance and curriculum — each [[species]] teaches [[chemistry]], [[ecology]], and [[evolution]] through direct observation. the knowledge graph catalogs over 500 species with their medicinal compounds, nutritional profiles, and cultivation requirements

plants produce [[antimicrobial]], [[anti-inflammatory]], and [[anticancer]] compounds as defense mechanisms. these secondary metabolites — [[alkaloids]], [[flavonoids]], [[terpenes]] — form the basis of both traditional and modern pharmacology
