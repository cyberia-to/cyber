SI unit of [[power]], equal to one [[joule]] per second

named after James Watt, who improved the steam engine

measures the rate of [[energy]] transfer or conversion

1 horsepower ~ 746 watts

human basal metabolic rate: ~80 watts

typical household light bulb: 10-100 watts

[[Sun]] luminosity: 3.846e26 watts

kilowatt (kW) for appliances, megawatt (MW) for power plants, gigawatt (GW) for national grids

electrical power: P = [[volt]] times [[ampere]] (voltage times current)
