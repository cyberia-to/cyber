# drinks

Liquid preparations consumed for hydration, pleasure, or health.

Includes [[tea]], [[coffee]], juices, fermented beverages, and [[herbal]] infusions.

Fermented [[drinks]] such as kombucha, kefir, and tepache provide probiotics that support digestive health.

Fresh juices from tropical fruits deliver concentrated vitamins and [[antioxidant]] compounds.

[[Herbal]] infusions made from [[medicinal]] plants serve as gentle daily tonics in traditional wellness practice.

Beverage selection within a [[diet]] reflects both cultural preference and the available harvest from local [[permaculture]] systems.
