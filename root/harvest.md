gathering of mature [[crops]] from the field

timing is critical: too early reduces yield, too late causes loss to weather, pests, shattering

methods: hand picking, sickle, combine harvester, mechanical shaker

follows the seasonal rhythm dictated by [[photosynthesis]] and climate

post-harvest: drying, threshing, winnowing, storage, processing

storage losses (insects, mold, rodents) can destroy 30% of [[grain]] in developing regions

[[fermentation (food)]], milling, and preservation extend harvest value through time

celebration of harvest is universal across cultures: thanksgiving, Sukkot, Pongal

links [[agriculture]] to economy: surplus enables trade, cities, and civilization
