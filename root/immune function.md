the capacity of the [[immune system]] to detect and neutralize [[pathogen]]s, foreign substances, and aberrant cells

immune function operates through innate barriers (skin, mucosa, [[inflammation]]) and adaptive responses (antibodies, T-cells, immunological memory)

[[nutrition]], [[sleep]], [[stress]], and [[microbiome]] composition modulate immune performance

many [[plant]]s support immune function, including [[garlic]], [[echinacea]], [[elderberry]], and [[astragalus]], through [[antimicrobial]] and immunomodulatory [[secondary metabolite]]s
