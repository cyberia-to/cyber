device that moves fluids (liquids or gases) by mechanical action

types: centrifugal, positive displacement (piston, gear, peristaltic), vacuum

centrifugal: rotating impeller ([[wheel]]) converts kinetic [[energy]] to pressure

positive displacement: traps fixed volume and forces it through outlet

critical for [[irrigation]], municipal [[water]] supply, industrial processes

the heart is a biological pump: four-chamber positive displacement

powered by [[engine]] or electric motor

hydraulic systems use pumps to transmit force through confined fluid

vacuum pumps enabled the discovery of [[semiconductor]] physics and space [[telescope]] optics

[[energy]] cost of pumping is a major factor in [[agriculture]] and [[construction]]
