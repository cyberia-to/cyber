pathological conditions affecting the integumentary system — infections (bacterial, fungal, viral), inflammatory conditions (eczema, psoriasis), and wounds. skin is the body's largest organ and its first defense barrier

many [[species]] produce compounds effective against skin pathogens. [[antibacterial]] agents from [[garlic]] and neem treat bacterial infections. [[antifungal]] compounds from tea tree and [[rosemary]] address fungal skin conditions. [[anti-inflammatory]] [[flavonoids]] and [[terpenes]] reduce dermatitis symptoms

traditional medicine across cultures uses plant-based topical preparations for [[wound healing]], burn treatment, and skin infection management. the [[cyber valley]] species catalog documents these applications with their active compounds
