simultaneous purchase and sale of an asset across different markets to profit from price differences

core mechanism driving market efficiency: arbitrageurs close price gaps, aligning values across venues

risk-free arbitrage: guaranteed profit from mispricing, theoretically eliminated instantly in efficient markets

statistical arbitrage: probabilistic exploitation of historical price relationships

triangular arbitrage: exploiting inconsistencies among three currency pairs

in [[DeFi]]: cross-chain and cross-pool arbitrage bots maintain price consistency across [[AMM]] liquidity pools

arbitrage connects fragmented markets into a unified price discovery system

transaction costs, latency, and [[gas]] fees set the lower bound for exploitable price differences
