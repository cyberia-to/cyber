Co-developed authenticated data structures with [[Michael Goodrich]], providing cryptographic guarantees for data integrity in untrusted environments.

Pioneered authenticated skip lists and hash-based verification schemes used in certificate revocation, outsourced databases, and blockchain state proofs.

Research bridges computational geometry, graph drawing, and [[cryptography]].
