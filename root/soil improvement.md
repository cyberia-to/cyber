# soil improvement

The practice of enhancing soil fertility, structure, and biological activity to support productive plant growth.

Methods include [[composting]] organic matter, [[mulching]] to retain moisture and suppress weeds, cover cropping with [[nitrogen-fixing]] plants, and biochar application for long-term carbon sequestration. Each technique addresses different aspects of soil health: chemical nutrient balance, physical structure, and microbial diversity.

In [[cyber valley]], soil improvement is a continuous process. Tropical soils lose nutrients rapidly through leaching, so building organic matter and maintaining living root systems year-round is essential. [[Legumes]], [[leucaena]] leaf mulch, and kitchen compost form the primary nutrient cycling pathways.

Healthy soil is the foundation of any [[permaculture]] system and the basis for food [[sovereignty]].
