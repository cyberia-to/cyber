# family

A taxonomic rank between [[order]] and [[genus]] that groups related genera sharing common ancestry.

In [[botany]], family names end in -aceae (e.g. Fabaceae for legumes, Rosaceae for roses). In [[zoology]], family names end in -idae. Each family contains one or more [[genera]] united by key morphological and genetic traits.

Families represent a practical level of classification for field identification. Recognizing the family of a [[plant]] or [[animal]] often reveals shared characteristics like flower structure, fruit type, or behavioral patterns.

Linnaeus established the foundation, and modern [[phylogenetics]] refines family boundaries using molecular data.
