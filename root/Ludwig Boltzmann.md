1844-1906. Austrian physicist and philosopher.

Founded [[statistical mechanics]], connecting microscopic particle behavior to macroscopic [[thermodynamics]].

Derived the entropy formula S = k * ln(W), linking [[entropy]] to the number of accessible microstates.

Introduced the Boltzmann distribution, describing energy distribution across particles in thermal equilibrium.

His statistical interpretation of [[entropy]] bridges [[thermodynamics]], [[information theory]], and [[computation]].

The Boltzmann constant k is a fundamental physical constant connecting temperature to energy.

His framework applies wherever large numbers of agents produce emergent order, from gas molecules to [[knowledge graph]] link distributions.
