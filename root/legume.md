nitrogen-fixing plants of family Fabaceae: beans, lentils, peas, chickpeas, soybeans

symbiotic root nodules harbor Rhizobium bacteria converting atmospheric N2 to ammonia

primary plant protein source for human nutrition

enriches [[soil]] nitrogen, essential rotation partner for [[grain]] and other [[crops]]

[[seed]] pods split along two seams when mature

processed via cooking, sprouting, [[fermentation (food)]] (tempeh, miso, soy sauce)

low water demand relative to animal protein, efficient use of [[energy]]

central to [[food sovereignty]] and [[clean food]] systems
