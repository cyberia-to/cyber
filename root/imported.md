# imported

A source category in [[cyber valley]] recipes. Imported ingredients are those obtained from outside the local growing region — oils, spices, grains, and specialty items.

Many staple cooking ingredients such as [[olive oil]], [[black pepper]], and [[cinnamon]] originate in distant climates and require import.

Tracking the [[imported]] label in recipes helps measure self-sufficiency and plan future cultivation goals within [[cyber valley]].

Balancing [[imported]] goods with [[local farmers]] sourcing strengthens resilience and flavor diversity.
