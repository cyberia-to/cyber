a property of substances that inhibit or prevent the growth of [[cancer]] cells

mechanisms include induction of [[apoptosis]], inhibition of cell proliferation, and suppression of [[angiogenesis]]

many [[plant]]s contain anticancer [[compound]]s: [[curcumin]] from [[turmeric]], [[resveratrol]] from [[grape]]s, [[quercetin]] from [[onion]]s

closely related to [[antioxidant]] and [[anti-inflammatory]] properties
