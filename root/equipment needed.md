# equipment needed

Tools and equipment required for plant cultivation and maintenance at [[cyber valley]].

Hand tools include shovels, pruning shears, hoes, rakes, and grafting knives for daily garden operations.
Irrigation systems range from drip lines and soaker hoses to gravity-fed tanks and automated timers.
Processing equipment covers dehydrators, seed cleaners, cold-press oil extractors, and grain mills.
Soil testing kits and pH meters help maintain optimal growing conditions across all [[permaculture]] zones.
Protective gear such as gloves, knee pads, and sun hats ensures safe and sustained fieldwork.
Power tools like chainsaws and brush cutters support land clearing and [[food forest]] establishment.
