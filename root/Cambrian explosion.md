rapid diversification of multicellular animal life ~541 million years ago

most major animal phyla appeared within a ~25 million year window

origin of eyes, shells, exoskeletons, predation, bilateral symmetry

preceded by the Ediacaran biota: soft-bodied, sessile organisms

triggers: rising oxygen levels, [[calcium]] availability, ecological arms races

first appearance of arthropods, mollusks, chordates, echinoderms

the Burgess Shale and Chengjiang fossil beds preserve this radiation

deepest example of an [[epoch]] boundary driven by innovation cascades

analogous to the [[Information Age]] explosion of digital complexity
