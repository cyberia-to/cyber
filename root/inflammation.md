the body's [[immune system]] response to tissue damage, [[infection]], or irritants

acute inflammation is a protective mechanism: increased blood flow delivers [[white blood cell]]s to the affected area for healing

chronic inflammation persists over time and contributes to diseases including [[arthritis]], [[diabetes]], cardiovascular disease, and [[cancer]]

modulated by [[anti-inflammatory]] substances found in [[diet]], [[plant]]s, and [[medicine]]
