# terpenes

A large class of organic compounds produced by plants, especially conifers and aromatic herbs.

Responsible for aroma and flavor in essential oils, [[terpenes]] mediate plant defense, pollinator attraction, and allelopathic signaling.

Built from isoprene units (C5H8), they range from simple monoterpenes to complex triterpenes and beyond.

Common examples include limonene in citrus, pinene in pine [[bark]], and linalool in lavender.

[[Terpenoids]] are modified [[terpenes]] containing oxygen, found in resins, latex, and medicinal extracts.

In [[coffee]], [[terpenes]] contribute to the aromatic complexity revealed during roasting.
