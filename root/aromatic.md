a property of compounds containing ring structures with delocalized electrons, producing distinctive scents

in chemistry, aromatic compounds are cyclic molecules stabilized by [[electron]] delocalization across conjugated pi bonds, following Huckel's rule

in botany and [[herbalism]], aromatic [[plant]]s such as [[rosemary]], [[lavender]], and [[thyme]] produce volatile [[oil]]s rich in [[terpene]]s and [[phenol]]s

aromatic compounds serve ecological roles in pollinator attraction, [[antimicrobial]] defense, and [[allelopathy]]
