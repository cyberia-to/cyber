sound is a shared medium. [[cyberia]] manages it so that rest, work, and ecology all coexist.

## ambient limits by zone and time

| zone | day (07:00 – 22:00) | night (22:00 – 07:00) |
|------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| residential | 55 dB(A) | 40 dB(A) |
| public / commercial | 65 dB(A) | 50 dB(A) |
| productive / workshop | 75 dB(A) | prohibited |
| forest / conservation | 45 dB(A) | 35 dB(A) |

measured at the nearest affected boundary, not at the source.

## events and amplified music

- events above 70 dB(A) at the event perimeter require 48 h notice to all residents within 300 m
- amplified music outdoors: permitted 10:00 – 23:00, cutoff at 23:00 regardless of event duration
- subwoofer frequencies below 80 Hz: level-limited so that bass is not audible inside neighboring residential structures
- maximum two amplified events per week per venue in shared areas

## machinery and tools

- power tools: [[laba]] workshop zone only during 08:00 – 18:00
- generators: see [[air policy]] — engine noise constraint is 60 dB(A) at 7 m
- chainsaws and grinders: 09:00 – 17:00 only, minimum 50 m from residential structures

## ecology

bird and wildlife communication occurs primarily in the 2–8 kHz range. chronic noise above 55 dB(A) in forest and garden zones degrades nesting success and foraging behavior.

- loud machinery excluded from forest buffer zones at all times
- dawn chorus window (05:00 – 07:00): no amplified sound anywhere on the estate

## enforcement

- neighbor complaint: document location, time, and estimated level
- three documented complaints from separate residents → formal measurement by designated monitor within 14 days
- confirmed violation: source owner covers remediation costs (acoustic treatment, scheduling change)
