American computer scientist, professor at UC Irvine.

Developed efficient authenticated data structures with [[Roberto Tamassia]], enabling verifiable queries over outsourced data.

His work on skip lists and tree-based authentication schemes influenced the design of verifiable state in blockchain protocols.

Co-authored foundational textbooks on algorithm design and data structures.

Research spans [[cryptography]], data structures, and computational geometry.
