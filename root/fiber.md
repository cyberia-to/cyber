# fiber

Structural plant tissue composed of elongated cells with thick walls, providing mechanical support to stems and [[leaves]].

[[Dietary fiber]] aids [[digestion]] by adding bulk to food, promoting healthy gut [[microbiome]] activity, and regulating nutrient absorption.

Industrial [[fibers]] serve [[textile]] and [[construction]] uses, from [[cotton]] and [[hemp]] to engineered composites.

Plant [[fibers]] are rich in [[cellulose]], arranged in bundles that give [[wood]] and [[bark]] their tensile strength.

Soluble and insoluble forms play distinct roles in metabolism and intestinal health.
