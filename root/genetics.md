# genetics

The study of heredity and variation in living organisms. [[Genes]] encode biological information as [[DNA]] sequences, transmitting traits from parent to offspring.

Mendel's laws of segregation and independent assortment describe how alleles separate during gamete formation and recombine at fertilization. Modern [[molecular biology]] reveals the physical basis: DNA replication, transcription to RNA, and translation to protein.

Mutations — substitutions, insertions, deletions — introduce variation. Natural selection, drift, and gene flow act on this variation to drive [[evolution]] across generations.

Genomic tools such as sequencing and [[CRISPR]] editing now allow direct reading and rewriting of genetic instructions.
