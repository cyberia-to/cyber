number of oscillations or cycles per unit time

measured in [[hertz]] (Hz): 1 Hz = one cycle per second

inversely proportional to [[wavelength]]: f = v / lambda

audible sound: ~20 Hz to ~20,000 Hz

visible light: ~430 THz (red) to ~790 THz (violet)

radio frequencies span kHz to GHz, allocated by spectrum authorities

[[resonance]] occurs when a driving frequency matches a system's natural frequency

biological rhythms have characteristic frequencies: heartbeat (~1 Hz), [[brain]] waves (1-100 Hz)

sampling frequency must exceed twice the signal frequency ([[Nyquist theorem]])
