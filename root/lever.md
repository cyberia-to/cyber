rigid bar pivoting on a fulcrum, producing mechanical advantage

one of six simple machines, alongside [[wheel]], wedge, screw, pulley, inclined plane

three classes defined by relative positions of effort, load, and fulcrum

class 1: fulcrum between effort and load (seesaw, crowbar)

class 2: load between fulcrum and effort (wheelbarrow, nutcracker)

class 3: effort between fulcrum and load (tweezers, human forearm)

Archimedes: "give me a place to stand and I will move the Earth"

principle extends to hydraulic systems, [[pump]] mechanisms, and digital logic gates

[[energy]] input equals energy output minus friction losses
