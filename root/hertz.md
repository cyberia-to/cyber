SI unit of [[frequency]], equal to one [[oscillation]] per second

named after Heinrich Hertz, who first demonstrated electromagnetic waves

audible range: 20 Hz to 20 kHz

mains electricity: 50 Hz (most of the world) or 60 Hz (Americas, parts of Asia)

radio: kHz to GHz; Wi-Fi operates at 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz

processor clock speeds measured in GHz

musical standard pitch: A4 = 440 Hz

related to [[wavelength]] by f = v / lambda

refresh rate of displays measured in Hz: 60, 120, 144 Hz common
