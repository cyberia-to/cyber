Allium sativum, a bulbous [[plant]] of the [[onion]] family with strong [[antimicrobial]] and [[anti-inflammatory]] properties

garlic produces [[allicin]] upon crushing, a sulfur compound responsible for its pungent odor and broad-spectrum [[antibacterial]], [[antifungal]], and [[antiviral]] activity

used as both a [[culinary]] [[staple]] and a [[medicine]] for millennia, garlic supports [[cardiovascular health]], [[immune function]], and [[blood pressure]] regulation

garlic is cultivated worldwide and features prominently in [[traditional medicine]] across cultures
