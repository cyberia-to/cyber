a condition of frequent loose or watery bowel movements, often caused by infection, [[food]] intolerance, or [[inflammation]]

diarrhea results from impaired water absorption or excessive secretion in the intestinal lining

common infectious causes include [[bacteria]] such as [[Escherichia coli]], [[virus]]es such as [[rotavirus]], and [[parasite]]s such as [[giardia]]

[[traditional medicine]] employs [[astringent]] [[herbs]] and [[tannin]]-rich [[plant]]s such as [[blackberry]] leaf and [[psyllium]] for management, alongside oral rehydration
