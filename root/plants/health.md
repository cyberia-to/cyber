# health

Plants cultivated primarily for their health-promoting properties.

These [[species]] produce [[medicinal]] compounds, [[antifungal]] activity, [[antioxidant]] content, and [[wound healing]] capability.

Health plants form a core layer in [[cyber valley]] [[permaculture]] design, integrating pharmacy into the landscape.

Traditional knowledge systems and modern pharmacology both validate the therapeutic potential of locally grown health plants.

Cultivation priorities include species with multiple bioactive profiles that thrive in tropical and subtropical climates.

Regular harvest and processing transforms raw plant material into tinctures, teas, salves, and dietary supplements.
