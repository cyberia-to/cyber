wavelength:: 620-750 nm

emotion:: [[anger]]

the longest visible wavelength. highest energy in the warm spectrum

evolutionary signal: fire, blood, thermal injury, dominance display

## properties

- highest arousal of any color — accelerates heart rate, increases blood pressure
- most visible at distance in natural environments
- the color of iron oxide — rust, soil, Mars

## in nature

- warning coloration ([[aposematism]]): toxic frogs, venomous snakes, poisonous berries
- ripeness signal: fruits shift from green to red when ready for dispersal
- blood: the universal marker of injury and vitality
- fire: the oldest human tool and oldest human threat

## in [[prysm]]

- danger, critical failure, system overload, resource depletion
- the stop signal — immediate attention required
