the type system and semantic convention layer that emerges from [[cyberlink]] [[topology]]

## emergence

patterns of linking create implicit types, categories, and relationships — structure from connectivity. when many [[neurons]] consistently link [[particles]] of one kind to particles of another kind, a semantic convention crystallizes without explicit schema definition

## role in cyber

one of the 19 [[computation]] [[languages]] in [[cyber]]. neural language sits above raw [[cyberlinks]] and below formal [[languages]] like [[datalog]]. it captures the meaning that arises from repeated structural patterns in the [[cybergraph]]

## examples

- a [[particle]] linked by thousands of neurons as a source acquires the implicit type of a category or hub
- a pair of particles repeatedly co-linked acquires the implicit relation of synonymy or equivalence
- a cluster of particles densely interconnected acquires the implicit structure of a topic or domain

## relation to focus

the [[convergence vm]] computes [[focus]] over particles, but neural language defines what those focus values mean. a high-focus particle in a dense cluster signifies a core concept; a high-focus particle with sparse links signifies a gateway
