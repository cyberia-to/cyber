# animal feed

Plant material cultivated or harvested as nutrition for livestock and poultry.

Feed crops include grasses, legumes, grains, and root vegetables selected for high energy and protein content. Silage and hay preserve forage for seasons when fresh pasture is unavailable.

Balanced animal feed contains carbohydrates, proteins, fats, minerals, and [[vitamins]] matched to the dietary needs of each [[species]].

In [[cyber valley]], integrating feed production with crop rotation improves soil fertility and reduces [[imported]] inputs.

[[Fast growing]] grasses and nitrogen-fixing legumes form the backbone of sustainable feed systems.
