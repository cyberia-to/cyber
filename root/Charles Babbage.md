1791-1871. British mathematician, engineer, and inventor.

Designed the Difference Engine, a mechanical calculator for polynomial evaluation.

Designed the Analytical Engine, the first general-purpose [[computation]] machine with memory, processor, and conditional branching.

The Analytical Engine anticipated the von Neumann architecture by over a century.

Collaborated with [[Ada Lovelace]], who documented the machine's programming potential.

Father of the computer: his designs contain every essential element of modern digital machines.

Demonstrated that [[computation]] is a physical process that can be mechanized and automated.
