The product of [[mass]] and velocity — a conserved vector quantity describing motion.

conservation of momentum: total momentum in a closed system is constant

Newton's second law restated: [[force]] is the rate of change of momentum

central to [[mechanics]], collisions, and orbital dynamics

relativistic momentum increases without bound as velocity approaches light speed — see [[relativity]]

in [[quantum mechanics]], momentum is quantized and related to [[wave]]length via de Broglie relation

angular momentum: rotational analog, conserved independently

transfer of momentum is the mechanism of [[force]]
