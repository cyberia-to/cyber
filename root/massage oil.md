# massage oil

A carrier oil blended with [[essential oils]] for therapeutic body massage. Common bases include coconut, jojoba, and sweet almond oil.

The carrier oil provides glide and skin nourishment while diluting concentrated [[essential oils]] to safe topical levels.

Different blends target relaxation, muscle recovery, or circulation. [[Terpenes]] such as linalool and limonene contribute both scent and therapeutic effect.

Cold-pressed, unrefined carriers preserve vitamins and fatty acids that support skin health.

[[Avocado oil]] and [[olive oil]] serve as rich, accessible base options for homemade formulations.
