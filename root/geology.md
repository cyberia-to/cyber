# geology

the discipline that studies the earth as a physical and chemical system. geology bridges [[geo]] (territory, terrain, earth systems) and [[chemo]] (mineral composition, geochemistry, rock-forming reactions)

in the [[crystal]], geology spans two domains:

- [[geo]] — [[plate tectonics]], [[volcano]], [[earthquake]], [[continent]], [[ocean]], [[geological time]], [[soil]]
- [[chemo]] — mineral chemistry, crystallography, geochemical cycles

## branches

- petrology → [[geo]] + [[chemo]] (rock formation, igneous/sedimentary/metamorphic)
- mineralogy → [[chemo]] + [[geo]] (mineral identification, crystal structure)
- geophysics → [[geo]] + [[quantum]] (seismic waves, magnetic field, gravity surveys)
- paleontology → [[geo]] + [[bio]] (fossils, [[Cambrian explosion]], mass [[extinction event]])
- hydrogeology → [[geo]] (groundwater, [[water cycle]], aquifers)
- volcanology → [[geo]] ([[volcano]], [[agung]], [[merapi]], [[vesuvius]], [[etna]], [[fuji]])
