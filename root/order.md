arrangement of elements into a non-random pattern. order is the inverse of entropy — [[information]] exists where order exists. in [[cyber]], the [[cybergraph]] creates order from raw [[data]] by structuring [[particles]] through [[cyberlinks]]

the [[tri-kernel]] computes order from chaos: [[diffusion]] explores, [[springs]] enforce structure, and [[heat]] smooths. the fixed-point [[focus]] distribution is the protocol's measure of order — which [[particles]] matter, in what proportion

thermodynamic order requires energy to maintain. in [[cyber]], the energy is economic: [[neurons]] spend [[will]] and stake [[tokens]] to create [[cyberlinks]]. without this expenditure, the [[cybergraph]] would be noise. [[syntropy]] — the tendency toward increasing order — emerges from incentivized [[learning]]
