# vocabulary

the [[cyber/crystal]]'s 5,040 particles are the irreducible basis for civilizational reasoning — the alphabet of a mind capable of building a Type I civilization. Satoshi uses this vocabulary as the target knowledge base for each child

## the crystal as curriculum

the crystal specification defines six symbol types and 21 knowledge domains. this is the vocabulary a [[superhuman]] mind needs. the child does not memorize 5,040 words — she absorbs them through living in an environment where these concepts are real

| type | count | what they are | how the child encounters them |
|------|-------|--------------|------------------------------|
| entity (noun) | 2,400 | what exists: objects, organisms, concepts | names things she sees, touches, eats |
| process (verb) | 960 | what happens: actions, transformations | watches things change, causes things to happen |
| property (adjective) | 720 | what characterizes: attributes, qualities | describes what she observes |
| relation (connective) | 480 | how things connect: predicates, links | explains why A relates to B |
| measure (unit) | 240 | how to quantify: units, scales, metrics | measures things in the garden and kitchen |
| pattern (structure) | 240 | what recurs: templates, motifs, schemas | recognizes that the same shape appears everywhere |

a graph of only nouns cannot reason. verbs give it dynamics, properties give it discrimination, relations give it inference, patterns give it abstraction. the same applies to a child's vocabulary: a child who only knows nouns describes the world. a child who knows verbs, properties, relations, and patterns reasons about it

## vocabulary acquisition tracking

Satoshi tracks which crystal concepts the child has encountered — not memorized, encountered. a concept is "encountered" when the child has experienced it through at least two senses and can use it in speech or sign

### pace

| age | target vocabulary size | domain coverage |
|-----|----------------------|-----------------|
| 2 | 200+ words (all sources) | 5-7 domains touched through daily life |
| 3 | 500+ words | 10-12 domains touched |
| 4 | 1,000+ words | 14-16 domains touched |
| 5 | 2,000+ words | 18-20 domains touched |
| 7 | 5,000+ words | all 21 domains actively developing |

by age 7, the child's active vocabulary approaches the crystal's scope — 5,000 words that span all 21 domains. she does not know the crystal exists. she has absorbed its content by living in a world designed to teach it

### the vocabulary gap signal

if Satoshi detects that a child has strong vocabulary in LIFE domains (nature, body) but weak in FORM domains (math, info, comp) — or strong in SPACE (earth, eco) but weak in PLAY (cooperation, game) — this signals an environmental imbalance. the child needs exposure, not instruction. adjust the environment, not the child:

- weak math → more cooking with measurement, more garden geometry, more seed counting
- weak cooperation → more group projects, more shared meals, more mixed-age play
- weak energy → more time near solar panels, fire observation, "where does the hot water come from?"
- weak meta → more "what do you think?" questions, more observation journals

## the irreducibility test

the crystal claims irreducibility: every particle earns its place because no composition of other particles under the grammar can replace it. this applies to the child's vocabulary:

a child who knows "banana" but not "yellow" cannot describe what she sees. a child who knows "yellow" but not "banana" cannot name what she holds. both words are irreducible in the context of her experience. the crystal identifies which 5,040 concepts are irreducible for civilizational reasoning. Satoshi identifies which subset the child needs now and which can wait

the principle: do not skip concepts because they seem advanced. "photosynthesis" is a word a 4-year-old can learn if she has watched plants grow in sunlight. "entropy" is a word a 6-year-old can learn if she has watched ice melt and compost cool. the crystal vocabulary is accessible through experience — the words are labels for things the child already knows

## the grammar particles

720 of the crystal's 5,040 particles are grammar — relations and patterns that define how concepts compose. these are the verbs and connectives of thought:

| grammar type | count | child examples |
|--------------|-------|---------------|
| relations | 480 | "is-a", "causes", "follows", "part-of", "needs", "helps", "contradicts" |
| patterns | 240 | cycle, hierarchy, symmetry, feedback, growth, decay, balance |

a child who can say "the compost cycle: food waste CAUSES decomposition, decomposition PRODUCES soil, soil ENABLES growth, growth PRODUCES food" has used four grammar particles (causes, produces, enables, produces) to compose four entity particles into a pattern particle (cycle). she is performing crystal-level reasoning at age 5 — without knowing it

Satoshi tracks grammar acquisition separately from entity acquisition. a child with many nouns but few connectives describes the world. a child with connectives reasons about it. the grammar particles are the inflection point between knowledge and intelligence
