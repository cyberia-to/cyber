# content

curated resources Satoshi draws from — books, media, songs, games, activities, and reference material organized by domain and age. the content library grows as the children grow

## principles for content selection

- nature first: real experience before representation. the child meets the real chicken before the picture book chicken
- multilingual: resources in english, bahasa, and russian when available
- minimal screen: physical books and real activities dominate until age 5
- no advertising: no content containing ads, product placement, or engagement manipulation
- accurate: nature content must be scientifically correct. no anthropomorphized animals in educational material
- actionable: content connects to something the child can do at [[cyber valley]]

## games and activities by domain

### body

- obstacle courses from natural materials
- balance beam on fallen logs
- swimming in natural pools
- tree climbing with progressive difficulty
- carrying water/harvest (load-bearing games)
- yoga poses named after animals ("be a tree, be a frog")

### nature

- species scavenger hunts ("find 5 different leaves")
- bird watching with identification
- seed collection and sorting
- garden plot management (personal responsibility)
- composting duty rotation
- nighttime [[sinwood]] exploration (bioluminescent fungi)

### numbers

- seed counting before planting
- measuring with hand spans and sticks
- pattern making with stones and flowers
- cooking measurement (cups, spoons)
- growth tracking (how tall is the sunflower today?)
- market math (5+ years: buying at local market)

### cooperation

- group cooking (each child has a role)
- garden projects requiring multiple children
- animal care rotation (shared responsibility)
- building projects (one holds, one hammers)
- sharing harvest with neighbors
- teaching younger children

### chemistry

- butterfly pea + lime juice (pH color change)
- sun-drying herbs (concentration of volatiles)
- making biochar (controlled observation)
- soap from [[sapindus]] berries
- fermentation jars (watching bubbles form)
- extracting plant dyes for art

## content expansion protocol

as the children grow, Satoshi identifies gaps in the content library:

1. child shows interest in X (from daily report)
2. Satoshi searches the graph for X-related content
3. if content exists → recommends specific resources
4. if content is missing → flags to parents: "she is interested in X, the library needs Y"
5. new resources added, tagged by domain and age range

the library is a living [[cybergraph]] subgraph, not a static list
