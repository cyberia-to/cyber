# body

the first technology. before any tool, any language, any abstraction — the body. the [[superhuman]] vector of physical capability begins at birth

## why body is first

a child who masters her body trusts herself. trust in the body is the foundation for trust in the mind. a child who can climb, fall, recover, and climb again has learned the most important lesson: failure is information, not punishment. this is the same lesson that drives [[evolution]], [[convergent computation]], and the [[tri-kernel]]

at [[cyber valley]], the terrain itself is the gym: volcanic slopes, canyon edges, tree roots, river stones, mud, rain. no flat floors, no padding. the body learns to read terrain because the terrain demands it

## developmental tracking

### explorer (0-2 years)

motor milestones Satoshi tracks:
- rolling, crawling, cruising, first steps, confident walking on uneven ground
- grasping: palmar → pincer → precision (picking seeds, handling insects gently)
- climbing: furniture → low rocks → tree roots → low branches
- water: comfort in water, splashing, first assisted floating
- barefoot: walking on soil, grass, gravel, wet stone — different textures train proprioception

sensory milestones:
- responds to bird calls, rain sounds, wind
- distinguishes textures: smooth leaf vs rough bark vs wet mud
- tracks moving insects, falling water, swaying branches
- taste exploration: bitter leaf, sweet fruit, sour citrus, earthy turmeric

### builder (2-5 years)

- running on slopes, jumping from rocks, balancing on fallen logs
- carrying objects: baskets, water containers, harvest — load-bearing strength
- swimming: independent water confidence, floating, basic strokes
- tool use: [[loppers]], small [[shovel]], watering can, mortar and pestle
- fine motor: threading seeds, sorting leaves, drawing in soil
- endurance: walking 2+ km on forest trails without fatigue
- climbing: confident in trees, understands branch strength, self-assesses risk

### maker (5-7 years)

- sustained physical work: digging, building, harvesting for 30+ minutes
- complex coordination: cutting with knife, hammering, tying knots
- body awareness: names muscles, understands why exercise matters
- flexibility and strength basics: hanging, crawling, rolling, tumbling
- navigation: finds way through known forest paths independently

## health tracking

Satoshi monitors through caretaker reports:

| signal | healthy | concern |
|--------|---------|---------|
| sleep | regular rhythm, wakes alert | fragmented, resistant, groggy |
| appetite | varied, enthusiastic about food from garden | narrow preferences, low energy |
| energy | sustained through the day, natural wind-down | crashes, hyperactivity cycles |
| illness | quick recovery, rare episodes | frequent infection, slow recovery |
| skin | clear, resilient | persistent rashes, slow wound healing |
| digestion | regular, comfortable | frequent discomfort, irregularity |

nutrition source: [[organiq]] food from [[cyber valley]] gardens. the child eats what grows around her: [[banana]], [[papaya]], [[taro]], [[moringa]], [[turmeric]], [[guava]], [[coffee cherry]], [[avocado]], eggs from [[gallus gallus domesticus]]. nutrition tracking maps to [[species]] — which plants provide which compounds, connecting body to [[satoshi/nature]]

## the superhuman connection

every physical capability the child develops maps to the [[superhuman]] vision:

- proprioception → awareness of body in space → foundation for morphological adaptation
- immune resilience → superimmunity → [[dna repair mechanisms]]
- metabolic efficiency → advanced [[metabolism]] → energy autonomy
- sensory acuity → expanded perception → cross-species [[communication]]
- physical confidence → environmental mastery → operating in any terrain

the body is where [[superhuman]] begins. everything else builds on it
