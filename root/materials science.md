# materials science

the discipline that studies the properties of matter and how to engineer new materials. materials science bridges [[chemo]] (molecular structure determines properties) and [[tech]] (materials enable construction and machines)

in the [[crystal]], materials science spans two domains:

- [[chemo]] — [[polymerization]], crystal structure, phase transitions, [[solubility]], molecular bonding
- [[tech]] — [[metal]], [[glass]], [[wood]], [[bamboo]], [[bioplastic]], [[bioepoxy]], [[superwood]], [[semiconductor]]

## branches

- metallurgy → [[chemo]] + [[tech]] ([[metal]], alloys, hardness, corrosion)
- polymer science → [[chemo]] ([[polymerization]], plastics, [[cellulose]], [[bioplastic]])
- ceramics → [[chemo]] + [[tech]] (oxides, silicates, [[glass]], [[limestone]])
- biomaterials → [[chemo]] + [[bio]] (biocompatible implants, scaffolds)
- nanomaterials → [[chemo]] + [[quantum]] (quantum dots, nanotubes, surface effects)
- wood science → [[tech]] + [[bio]] ([[wood]], [[wood-density]], [[wood-durability]], [[superwood]], [[bamboo]])
