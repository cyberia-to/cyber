SI unit of amount of substance

one mole contains exactly 6.02214076e23 entities (Avogadro's number)

bridges the atomic and macroscopic scales: one mole of [[carbon]]-12 has a mass of exactly 12 grams

enables stoichiometry: balanced equations give mole ratios of reactants and products

molar mass (g/mol) equals the atomic or molecular mass expressed in daltons

molar volume of an ideal gas at STP: 22.4 liters

molarity (mol/L) is the standard concentration unit in solution chemistry

named by Wilhelm Ostwald, derived from the German Molekul
