- the unified science of [[cyber]]
- convergence of [[cybernetics]], [[physics]], [[mathematics]], and [[information theory]] into a single formal discipline
- the mother of all sciences from the perspective of [[superintelligence]]
- ## why unification
	- [[cybernetics]] provides the control loop: feedback, regulation, goal-directed behavior
	- [[physics]] provides the substrate: energy, entropy, conservation laws, spacetime
	- [[mathematics]] provides the language: proof, structure, computation, abstraction
	- [[information theory]] provides the currency: bits, compression, channel capacity, entropy
	- these four are not independent disciplines — they share deep [[isomorphism]]s
	- [[entropy]] in [[thermodynamics]] is [[entropy]] in [[information theory]] is uncertainty in [[probability]]
	- [[diffusion]] in physics is random walk in mathematics is message passing in networks
	- [[feedback loop]]s in cybernetics are fixed points in mathematics are [[equilibrium]] in physics
	- cybics recognizes these as one science viewed through different lenses
- ## scope
	- [[set theory]], [[graph theory]], [[linear algebra]], [[probability]], [[calculus]], [[topology]], [[logic]]
	- [[category theory]] — structure-preserving maps between domains
	- [[game theory]] — strategic interaction, mechanism design
	- [[number theory]] — primes, modular arithmetic, the basis of [[cryptography]]
	- [[mechanics]], [[thermodynamics]], [[electromagnetism]], [[quantum mechanics]], [[relativity]], [[cosmology]]
	- [[computation]] — Turing machines, complexity classes, halting problem
	- [[information theory]] — entropy, compression, channel capacity
	- [[distributed systems]] — consensus, Byzantine fault tolerance
	- [[machine learning]] — neural networks, training, inference, embeddings
	- [[cryptography]] — hashing, signatures, zero-knowledge proofs
- ## in the [[cyber/crystal]]
	- cybics is a pillar domain (2Q = 480 particles)
	- it absorbs what were previously separate domains: mathematics, physics, computer science
	- the unification reflects the protocol's own architecture: the [[tri-kernel]] is simultaneously a physical process (diffusion, springs, heat), a mathematical structure (operators, eigenvalues, fixed points), and a computation (iterative convergence)
