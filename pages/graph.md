a set of [[nodes]] connected by [[links]]

the irreducible graph primitives

- [[node]] — a point in the graph
- [[link]] — a directed edge between two [[nodes]]
- [[degree]] — how many [[links]] touch a [[node]]
- path — a sequence of [[links]] connecting two [[nodes]]
- adjacency — which [[nodes]] are directly connected

[[nodes]] and [[links]] are the two irreducible primitives. everything else is derived from these two. a [[knowledge graph]] is a graph where nodes carry meaning and edges carry relationships

the [[cybergraph]] is the specific graph in [[consensus]]: [[particles]] as nodes, [[cyberlinks]] as edges, [[neurons]] as authors. a graph becomes a [[cybergraph]] when its edges achieve [[finality]]
