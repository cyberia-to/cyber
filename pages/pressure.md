[[force]] per unit area, measured in [[pascal]] (Pa)

atmospheric pressure at sea level: ~101325 Pa (1 atm)

types: atmospheric, hydrostatic, osmotic, partial, vapor

hydrostatic pressure increases with depth in a fluid: P = rho * g * h

osmotic pressure drives [[water]] across semipermeable membranes in biological systems

governs gas behavior via [[Boyle's law]] (P inversely proportional to volume at constant [[temperature]])

critical in engineering: structural loads, hydraulics, pneumatics

measured by barometers, manometers, piezoelectric sensors
