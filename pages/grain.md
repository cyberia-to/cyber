cereal [[crops]]: wheat, rice, corn, barley, oats, sorghum, millet

foundation of civilization's caloric supply, storable for years

grass family ([[species]] Poaceae), domesticated across every continent

kernels contain endosperm (starch), germ (lipids, protein), bran (fiber)

processing: milling, malting, [[fermentation (food)]]

global staple: rice feeds Asia, wheat feeds Europe and the Middle East, corn feeds the Americas

requires [[irrigation]] at scale, depletes [[soil]] without rotation with [[legume]]

stored after [[harvest]], traded as the oldest commodity
