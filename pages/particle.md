content-addressed node in the [[cybergraph]]. identity = [[hash]] of content. immutable. exists or doesn't

the atom of the protocol. every file, keyword, image, model weight, or program reduces to a particle. hashing a file produces a particle — this act is a measurement that collapses "what content?" into a deterministic answer. once hashed, a particle is self-authenticating: anyone can verify its identity without trusting anyone

a particle is a unit of [[information]]. two linked particles form a [[cyberlink]]. the graph of all links is [[knowledge]]. the inferred structure of that graph is [[intelligence]]

CID format: (version, algorithm, parameters, field, digest)

```

```
