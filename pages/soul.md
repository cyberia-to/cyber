script attached to a [[neuron]] or [[particle]] with graph processing instructions

the programmable agent layer: a soul defines how a [[neuron]] responds to the [[cybergraph]]. when triggered by a [[signal]] or a change in [[state]], the soul executes its instructions — creating [[cyberlinks]], moving [[tokens]], or querying [[cyberank]]

a soul turns a passive [[neuron]] into an autonomous agent. a [[neuron]] with a [[spell]] has identity. a [[neuron]] with a soul has behavior. the spell signs, the soul decides what to sign

souls enable automated [[learning]]: agents that observe [[explicit knowledge]] from the [[tru]], derive [[implicit knowledge]], and encode it as new [[cyberlinks]] — closing the [[intelligence]] loop without human intervention
