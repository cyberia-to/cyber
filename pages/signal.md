a set of [[cyberlinks]] submitted by a [[neuron]] in a single [[step]]

a signal is the atomic unit of [[learning]] at the protocol level: one or more [[cyberlinks]] bundled into a transaction, signed, and committed to the [[cybergraph]] in one [[step]]

each [[cyberlink]] in a signal is a [[costly signal]] — it consumes [[focus]]. a signal groups related links that the [[neuron]] intends as a coherent statement

after a signal achieves [[finality]], the [[tru]] recomputes [[cyberank]] from the updated [[cybergraph]]

a [[neuron]] uses [[spell]] to [[sign]] and [[cast]] signals

see [[signal types]] for the full taxonomy of protocol messages
