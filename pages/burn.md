remove [[supply]] of a [[token]] from a [[neuron]] balance permanently

burning destroys [[tokens]]. the balance is debited and total [[supply]] decreases. burned [[tokens]] cannot be recovered

in [[cyber]] burning serves as a cost mechanism: creating [[cyberlinks]] burns [[will]], making each link a [[costly signal]]. this prevents spam and ensures the [[cybergraph]] reflects genuine [[attention]]

burn is the counterpart of [[mint]]. together they define the lifecycle of [[supply]]. [[pay]] moves balance, [[lock]] freezes it, [[update]] changes properties — burn ends it

burn is one of the [[plumb]] operations on [[tokens]]
