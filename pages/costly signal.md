a [[cyberlink]] that costs [[focus]] to create — making it an honest indicator of what the [[neuron]] values

the cost of [[learning]] is [[focus]]. [[focus]] regenerates proportionally to stake, and the total is conserved: emphasizing one thing defocuses others. a [[neuron]] cannot link everything — it must choose. this scarcity makes each [[cyberlink]] a costly signal

because linking costs [[focus]], the [[cybergraph]] accumulates weighted commitments rather than cheap assertions. the [[truth machine]] computes [[cyberank]] from these commitments — [[explicit knowledge]] emerges from the aggregate of costly signals

the economics: [[focus]] is the cost, [[cyberlink]] is the signal, [[cyberank]] is the outcome

see [[focus]] for the conservation law. see [[learning]] for the act of creating a costly signal. see [[knowledge theory]] for the epistemic framing
