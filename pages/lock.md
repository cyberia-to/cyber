a locked balance cannot be transferred until the lock expires. locking is how [[neurons]] commit [[tokens]] to the protocol — [[consensus]] participation, [[focus]] generation, and [[learning incentives]] all require locked stake

locking transforms liquid [[coins]] into committed weight. the locked amount generates [[attention]] and [[will]] — the two resources a [[neuron]] spends to create [[cyberlinks]]. without locking, a [[neuron]] has no influence on the [[cybergraph]]

lock is one of the [[plumb]] operations on [[tokens]]. [[pay]] moves balance, [[mint]] creates it, [[burn]] destroys it, lock freezes it
