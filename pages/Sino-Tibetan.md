second largest [[language]] family by number of speakers: ~1.3 billion

branches: Sinitic (Mandarin, Cantonese, Wu, Min, Hakka) and Tibeto-Burman (Tibetan, Burmese, Bodo, Tamang)

Mandarin Chinese: most spoken [[language]] on Earth (~920 million native speakers)

tonal languages: pitch distinguishes meaning (Mandarin has 4 tones, Cantonese has 6-9)

Chinese [[writing system]] is logographic: hanzi characters represent morphemes, shared across mutually unintelligible spoken varieties

literary tradition spanning 3,400+ years from oracle bone inscriptions through classical Chinese

Chinese characters influenced Japanese kanji, Korean hanja, Vietnamese chu Nom

the Sinitic branch preserves one of the longest continuous [[writing (invention)]] traditions in human history
