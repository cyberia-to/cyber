change [[state]] of a [[token]] or [[particle]]

update modifies properties of an existing on-chain object without creating or destroying it. for [[cards]]: metadata changes, ownership transfers, binding to new [[particles]]. for [[coins]]: parameter adjustments through [[consensus]] governance

update is one of the [[plumb]] operations on [[tokens]]. [[pay]] moves balance, [[mint]] creates, [[burn]] destroys, [[lock]] freezes, update changes properties in place

every update requires authorization — either a [[signature]] from the owner or a governance decision through [[consensus]]
