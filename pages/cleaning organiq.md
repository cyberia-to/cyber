guideline on how to maintain organiq 🍀properly.

  rule 📜:
- each thing has its own place 🗂️

	  fifo (first in – first out 🔄🔄):
	- products and materials are used in the order they arrive 📦
	- items that arrived or were prepared earlier are used first ⏳
	- new stock is placed behind the older stock so older items are used first 🔙
	- helps prevent spoilage and waste ♻️

chairs 🪑

- must be clean and maintained ✨

table 🍽️

- no dust 🧹
- no empty jars 🫙
- fresh flowers 🌸
- clean knives 🔪
- no dirty or wet cloths 🪣
- no dirty dishes (after serving food, check every 15–20 min; guests must not sit surrounded by dirty dishes) ⏱️

shelves

- must be clean 🧽
- labeled (without typos) 🏷️
- no spoiled fruits or vegetables (all that have been stored too long must be cooked or dried; use fifo rule) 🍏
- no cobwebs 🕸️

trash 🗑️

- trash bin must be clean ✨
- labeled: plastic, paper, glass, metal ♻️
- all food scraps must be put into the compost pile (exception: orange peels, all seeds) 🌱

under the table 📦

- boxes must be checked weekly for mold, dirt, or spoiled items 🔍
- weekly cleaning 🧼
- must store:
	- 2 clean towels 🪢🧖‍♀️
	- 2 clean cloths 🧻
	- 1 liter soap 🧴
	- 1 pack baking powder 🥣
	- 1 liter saucepan with lid 🍲
	- frying pan with lid (max 20 cm) 🍳
	- teapot 🫖
	- coffee maker ☕
	- first aid kit (check biweekly; replace expired items and restock if needed) ⛑️💉
	- warm clothes 🧥
	- teas and snacks 🍪
	- empty bottles for water (clean and dry) 🍼
	- tools 🛠️
	- chargers 🔌
	- one new water gallon 💧

	  near garden 🌱

must be cared for monthly:

- pruning ✂️
- mulching 🍂
- propagation 🌿
- feeding with compost or fertilizer if needed 🌾

mulch around 🍂

- must be clean (no plastic or fruit peels) 🚯
- must cover the soil properly 🌍

beanbags 🛋️

- clean every day 🧼
- pillowcases must be washed monthly 🛏️

fireplace 🔥

- must be clean 🧽
- keep under a roof (water causes rust) ⛱️

toilet 🚽

- clean the toilet every day 🧽
- make sure there is no green buildup on the handheld shower 🚿
- the toilet remote control must be clean 🎛️
- change the towel in the toilet every two weeks 🪢

shower 🚿

- water the plants every day 🌱
- shelves and mirror must be clean 🪞
- the soap bottle must be full and clean 🧴
- change the towel every two weeks and shake it out from insects every day 🐜
