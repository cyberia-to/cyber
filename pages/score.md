[[token]] type that is fungible and immovable

a score can be accumulated and compared but cannot be transferred between [[neurons]]. it stays with whoever earned it. this makes scores the natural form for reputation, contribution measures, and non-transferable credentials

in [[cyber]] the primary score is [[karma]] — aggregate [[focus]] earned by a [[neuron]] across all [[particles]] it has linked. high [[karma]] means sustained alignment with collective [[attention]]

scores sit in the [[token]] type matrix: [[coins]] are fungible and movable, [[cards]] are unique and movable, scores are fungible and immovable, [[badges]] are unique and immovable
