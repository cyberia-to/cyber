instrument for observing objects too small for the unaided eye

optical (light) microscope: magnification up to ~1500x, resolves to ~200 nm

electron microscope: transmission (TEM) and scanning (SEM), resolves to atomic scale

scanning probe: atomic force microscope (AFM), scanning tunneling microscope (STM)

fluorescence microscopy: labels specific molecules in living cells

revealed the cellular basis of life, bacteria, viruses, [[species]] microstructure

enabled [[semiconductor]] fabrication inspection and quality control

cryo-electron microscopy resolves protein structures without crystallization

complementary to [[telescope]]: one reaches inward, the other outward

essential for biology, materials science, and nanotechnology
