# Cyberia vision: re-design of civilization
title:: cyberia vision

In an era where digital nomads like us roam the globe searching for community, sustainability, and innovation, we've poured our hearts into creating a bold new project emerging from the lush volcanic slopes of Bali. 

Cyberia — is our vision for a network of "future cities" designed to harness collective intelligence through collective learning and coordination algorithms, achieve sovereignty in essential resources, and create permanent tribes for those weary of fleeting pop-up experiences.
At its heart is Cyber Valley, our 37-hectare pilot site in northern Bali.

And [Burn.City](https://burn.city/), our upcoming "moonly" event set to kickstart this cyber state from February 17 to March 20, 2026. Drawing on our cypherpunk roots, blockchain enablers, and a philosophy of vertical integration, we're aiming to redefine how we live, work, and thrive in the age of superintelligence.

- ## The Philosophy: Cyber State is Collective Intelligence
  
  Our philosophy for Cyberia is rooted in the interplay between collective intelligence and the creation of a cyber state. 
  
  > Any Cyber State eventually will acquire Collective Intelligence
  > Any Collective Intelligence eventually will own the land, hence become Cyber State
  
  So these two things are inevitably convergent.
  
  The idea is underpinned by the [Collective Focus Theorem](/afmAr6ThQ7SfNIn8Yk76_g), which proves that token-weighted random walks in fully authenticated graphs converge to a unique stationary distribution — essentially, a mathematical foundation for large-scale coordination and decentralized decision-making. CFT is the result of 10 years' research led by [@mastercyb](https://x.com/mastercyb) which is the missing building block for building truly intelligent collective systems.
  
  To harness collective intelligence in the Cyberia future, we envision integrating advanced collective learning and coordination algorithms seamlessly across the entire ecosystem. The [cyber protocol](https://cyber.page/) will serve as the foundation — enabling unified, provable knowledge accumulation through decentralized graphs. This directly extends what we built with [Bostrom](https://cyb.ai/) since 2021: the world's first decentralized knowledge graph on Cosmos SDK, explicitly designed as the bootloader for superintelligence.
  
  Every piece of content receives an eternal IPFS CID; every meaningful connection becomes an immutable [cyberlink](https://cyber.page/cyberlink/) on-chain. These links form a global graph ranked by token-weighted PageRank ([cyberank](https://cyber.page/cyberank)), delivering search that is censorship-resistant, manipulation-proof by design, and truly collective — the real decentralized alternative to the centralized Internet. 
  
  Bostrom is powered by $BOOT staking, "mint and burn" mechanics of Hydrogen (H) for base learning tokens: Volt (V for indexing) & Ampere (A for ranking), on-chain GPU compute for collective learning, and CosmWasm smart contracts for dApps on top of the graph. Over four years it has survived upgrades to v7, outages, and community recoveries, continues producing blocks, and has already accumulated ~17 million bits of negentropy — measurable order imposed on the chaos of human knowledge.

- ## Cypherpunk Ethos
  
  Juan Benet recently [distilled the cypherpunk methodology](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B08IXHLOQp4&list=PLSsVHWrO8Yh06WlNcYKtIDMjPiUXJjADu) into six principles: build utopias and protopias, enable secure and private communications, make money to develop and fund, face legal battles when necessary, build together, and solve open problems. Cyberia implements this framework through direct action rather than manifestos.
  
  We build on a three-layer sovereignty structure:
- L1 (Ethereum),
- L2 (Marshall Islands non-profit, CAP token, holding L3 shares),
- and L3 (local companies holding land titles, e.g. PT PMA in Indonesia). 
  
  This architecture — the world's first cyberstate fund — enables instant global access to capital, regulatory compliance, and tokenized governance while maintaining sovereignty at every level. It solves the open problem of scaling across digital, legal, and jurisdictional domains simultaneously.
  
  Economic sustainability comes through extreme vertical integration. Cyber Valley's 37 hectares demonstrate this practically: we harvested 1 tonne of coffee cherries this season. Raw value: $1/kg. Roasted: $10/kg. In the cup: $500/kg. Conventional systems spread this crazy 50x margin across a supply chain leaving farmers poor and starbucks rich. By controlling the entire chain from soil to cup, we capture margins that traditional models leak to intermediaries — correcting systemic inequity where growers earn the least. 
  
  Another example: remote land is cheap. $1k can buy 1 ha of land anywhere in the world. This is enough to sustain a family. This land is usually resource-rich, nature-strong, beautiful. However the market pricing this land as something invaluable. If just 20 families invest $50k each to build their dream and decide to live there - the land around will gain 50x immediately. So the problem boils down to a [coordination on the crowdinvested cake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fair_cake-cutting).
  
  The same principle applies across our entire stack: land acquisition, modular construction, food production, events, infrastructure. We strive to follow first principles to identify civilization-level bugs and fix them.
  
  This model funds continued [open source development](https://github.com/orgs/cyberia-to). So we may be the first open source development (yep, we mean physical building) company in existence.

- ## Soil, Biodiversity, and Growing: The Existential Foundation
  
  At the core of Cyberia's sustainability is our deep respect for soil, biodiversity, and regenerative growing practices—these are existential pillars for any future civilization. Soil is the skin of the Earth, underpinning 95% of global food production, filtering water, storing carbon to combat climate change, and harboring immense biodiversity. Healthy soil production is vital for human civilization, as it sustains permaculture, prevents erosion, regulates the water cycle, and supports ecosystems that have enabled societies to thrive for millennia. In Burn.City, our "burn to biochar" ethos transforms waste into nutrient-rich amendments, rebuilding topsoil that's been depleted globally at alarming rates. Some of our land was agricultural farmland where the previous farmer grew carrots in a monocultural way with chemical pesticides and herbicides.  
  
  [We did a soil test](https://cyber.page/soil) on our land, where the previous farmer grew carrots in a monoculture. These heavy metals appear later on your plates and as a result in your bodies. We aren't using this land for food production. We're focusing on remediation now. This is our case: in different countries the situation varies, but we bet the whole of Bali suffers from the same issue because many farmers use the same pesticides, herbicides, and fungicides.
  
  ![andosols](https://jade-gentle-pony-196.mypinata.cloud/ipfs/bafybeid72gqcsde7jehqfn7lcw4ecqlrazcdugyuuuhcbhh432jlruk37q) 
  Cyber Valley's soil is andosol — the best soil type in the world for natural and regenerative growing. Andosols are naturally fertile, with high organic matter accumulation, excellent water-holding capacity (like a sponge), efficient nutrient retention via high cation exchange, low bulk density for easy tilling, and good drainage—ideal for sustainable farming without heavy inputs. This allows us to focus on permaculture and efficient methods tailored to the Gesing region, yielding abundant harvests while enhancing soil health.
  
  ![knowledge graph](https://jade-gentle-pony-196.mypinata.cloud/ipfs/bafybeih7piv46ldqmcrji7lomie63enrnr7swl3khagge5sjvitkmj6xqq)
  
  Our biodiversity is thriving: over 500 plant species, 100+ birds, 50+ mushrooms, 20+ animals, including unique ones like the barking deer. The glowing forest (Sinwood) features bioluminescent fungi across 200 points, a natural wonder that underscores our site's ecological richness.
  
  ![bioluminescence](https://jade-gentle-pony-196.mypinata.cloud/ipfs/bafkreicdfbwgsdnyjwy76tub26o4xoncpjqoostxnwjepmkmjsljgv6zn4)
  
  We've planted, improved existing gardens and harvested a diverse array of crops, emphasizing perennial and healing plants: 500 kg avocados, 1 tonne  coffee, 140 kg taro, and more. New discoveries include black sapote, olives, and various herbs, all grown using low-impact techniques that build soil rather than deplete it.
  
  This growing perspective—regenerative, biodiverse, soil-centric—is key to our vision. By producing biochar and fostering natural systems, we're not just sustaining Cyberia; we're modeling how civilizations can regenerate the planet's most precious resource.
