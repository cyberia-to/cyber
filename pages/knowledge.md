[[neurons]] [[link]] [[particles]] in [[time]]

data is raw bytes. hashing data produces a [[particle]] — [[information]]. naming a particle with `~` makes it a [[file]]. linking [[particles]] to each other creates knowledge

a [[cyberlink]] is the atomic unit of knowledge: a signed, weighted, timestamped commitment that two [[particles]] are related. the sum of stored [[cyberlinks]] forms the knowledge of a [[neuron]]. the [[cybergraph]] is the knowledge of all [[neurons]]

knowledge is [[explicit knowledge]] — what was directly stated. the [[truth machine]] derives [[intelligence]] from knowledge through [[inference]]. intelligence is [[implicit knowledge]] — what can be inferred beyond what was stated

| concept | what | alias |
|---|---|---|
| [[knowledge]] | the graph as written by [[neurons]] | [[explicit knowledge]] |
| [[intelligence]] | what the [[truth machine]] computes from the graph | [[implicit knowledge]] |

the two processes:

| process | who | input | output |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[learning]] | [[neurons]] | [[particles]] | [[knowledge]] |
| [[inference]] | [[truth machine]] | [[knowledge]] | [[intelligence]] |

in machine [[learning]] terminology: learning is training, inference is inference. the [[cybergraph]] is the model. [[neurons]] train it by linking. the [[truth machine]] runs inference over it

every [[cyberlink]] records [[three basic arguments]]

- [[who]]: which [[neuron]] linked
- [[when]]: in which block
- [[what]]: which [[particles]] were linked

Shannon's [[information]] theory has no concept of knowledge — it deals only with transmission of symbols. knowledge begins where Shannon ends: when an agent asserts that two pieces of information are related, spending [[focus]] to do so. cheap talk produces noise. costly signals produce knowledge

the chain: data → [[information]] → [[file]] → [[knowledge]] → [[intelligence]]
