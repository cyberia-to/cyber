# best money

You hold money because it lasts. You send it because it moves. You trust it because you know how it is born.

CYB is born when the network actually learns — proven growth of [[knowledge]], not a treasury. You spend it on intelligence: compute, storage, the pipe, and [[inference]]. That is **smart**.

Once it is yours it stays yours. Time does not eat it. Nobody sees the balance. Nobody freezes the account. Eighteen million, written in the field. That is **safe**.

Then it goes. A second to final. A name, not an address. A phone, not a datacenter. Anyone — human, agent, animal — no KYC, no government in the way. That is **easy**.

Fairness, truth, intelligence — each turn makes the next stronger. The more the network learns, the better the money. That is **compounding**.

Inflation bootstraps an empty graph. Use replaces it: a commission on every move, split three ways. A third burns — a dividend to everyone who holds. A third to those who create value. A third to public goods the [[cybergraph]] names. That is **sustainable**.

Call it complete when those jobs are live. Liquidity and acceptance come after, from other people. They are verdicts. They are not the coin.
