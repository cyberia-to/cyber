# the history of cyber

The history of cyber is the road of the two networks that carry its promise: it starts empty, with no first life to bury.

| when | what |
|---|---|
| 2016 | the idea arrives as cyberChain |
| 2018 | the euler network puts a rank over a graph inside consensus; its tokens are test tokens |
| 2021-11-05 | bostrom begins as the bootloader. Its genesis distributes two tokens by one structure: BOOT, the coin of the bootloader, and TOCYB, the promise of the coin of cyber |
| 2022 | space-pussy begins beside it |
| 2026-06-14 | space-pussy is halted at height 19,940,993, at 08:36:41 UTC ([the old chain](https://github.com/cyberia-to/space-pussy/blob/main/docs/the-old-chain.md), space-pussy `genesis/sources.toml`) |
| 2026-08-05 | bostrom is halted at height 25,120,712 with 281,405,532,467,645 TOCYB in its bank |
| 2026-08-28 | both states are buried, every file pinned |

## the promise

TOCYB said one thing: hold it, and receive CYB when the network arrives. 30 % of it entered the genesis of 2021 and 70 % stayed in the gift that was never fully claimed. It is carried into the new bostrom unchanged, and at the genesis of cyber every TOCYB claims 666 [[cyber/$CYB|CYB]]. The rules that mint it: [[genesis/README|the genesis of cyber]].

## the order

pussy first, as the canary; bostrom, the bootloader; cyber last. The two elder networks are reborn from their burials with their balances and their cyberlinks. cyber starts empty and reads them.

## this repository

The repository cyber is three things at once: the graph of the protocol behind cyber.page, the cargo package `true-cyber` with the client `cyber` ([[docs/run-a-node|run a node of cyber]]), and, by this layout, the network repository of cyber. For a time the package and the network lived in a repository of their own, true-cyber; they came home on 2026-11-22.
