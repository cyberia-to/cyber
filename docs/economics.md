# the economy of CYB

What CYB is for, what is proposed and what is still open. Nothing of it runs: cyber is not launched and the node knows no network by that name.

## the job

CYB is the coin of cyber, the root money. It is one of three coins with three jobs ([tokens](https://cyber.page/tokens/)):

| | CYB | BOOT | PUSSY |
|---|---|---|---|
| network | cyber | bostrom | pussy |
| job | root money | bootloader | community |
| purpose | hard money | small kernel | fast growth |

cyber arrives last, after pussy and bostrom have proven the machine. Its money must be as hard as arithmetic, so nothing is tried on it: its emission is fixed by time, its cap by the field, and its parameters do not change by vote.

## running

Nothing. The promise of CYB runs instead: TOCYB, a token of bostrom, carried into the genesis of the new bostrom unchanged.

## proposed

The full text with its charts: [CYB](https://cyber.page/cyber/$cyb/).

| | proposed |
|---|---|
| the cap | p = 2⁶⁴ − 2³² + 1 = 18,446,744,069,414,584,321, the order of the Goldilocks field: arithmetic, with no vote over it |
| the supply at genesis | 187,416,084,623,451,570 CYB, about 1 % of the cap: every TOCYB at the halt of bostrom, 281,405,532,467,645, times 666 |
| how it enters | by claims: a holder proves the TOCYB balance in the state of bostrom and mints CYB |
| emission | by the network's age alone: M(t) = p · (1 − (1 + t/τ)^−k), τ = 0.33 year, k = 0.5; no steps and no halvings; about half of the cap in the first year |
| who receives it | work: mining and active stake, split by how much is staked; a passive stake earns rank and no income |
| the tax on movement | 1 % of every transfer, lock and unlock; half is burned, half goes to the budget of rewards |
| the direction of supply | down when the burn exceeds emission |
| writing a link | a lock |
| a neuron's weight | the square root of its stake |
| parameters | fixed |

## open

What they settle there is what cyber takes;

- the schedule above is written with real numbers; the node computes in the field, so its exact form in integers is to be fixed with the genesis.

The law: [tru specs/rewards.md](https://github.com/cyberia-to/tru/blob/master/specs/rewards.md). The long study of the budget's control: [adaptive hybrid consensus economics](https://github.com/cyberia-to/cyber/pull/161).
