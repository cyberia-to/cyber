# rune

Dynamic evaluation language for [[cyber]]. Rs syntax + Hoon's subject model + cybermark's sigil layer; one language, one AST, two syntactic registers — classic (familiar) and pure (alien) — lowering directly to [[nox]]. Adds `hint` (event stream), `eval` (runtime formulas), and `host` (external compute) extensions above [[trident]]'s 14 substrate languages.
