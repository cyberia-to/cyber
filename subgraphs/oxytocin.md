# oxytocin

500 sqm wellness and lifestyle space in Sayan, Ubud. four-stage build-out that collapses café, restaurant, spa, coworking, childcare, and life-logistics into one operator and one membership. the name is the molecule of trust and bonding — the space is engineered to produce it.

stages: thermal circuit anchor (stage-1) → coffee bar and deli (stage-2) → restaurant, market, universal chair zone, retail (stage-3) → coworking, kids, services layer (stage-4). each stage cash-flow positive before the next is committed. maturity target: ~$10,000/day gross, $90–100k/month net.
