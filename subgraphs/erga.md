# erga

Zero-copy ERGO (Autolykos v2) miner for Apple Silicon. Feminine of ἔργον
(ergon, "work") — the works/deeds. Built on [[honeycrisp]] (acpu, aruminium,
unimem) following the [[mona]] and [[zoya]] patterns.

Hypothesis: a honeycrisp-native Autolykos v2 implementation can exceed
MacMetal Miner's 60.3 MH/s on M4 Max and reach ~2.0 MH/W energy efficiency,
beating RTX 3090's best-documented 1.64 MH/W and making Apple Silicon the
efficiency-leading platform for ERGO PoW.

See `plan.md` in the repo for the phased verification plan.
