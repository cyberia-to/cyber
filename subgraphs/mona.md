# mona

RandomX miner for Apple Silicon. Workspace with `crates/randomx`, `crates/worker`, and `crates/mona`. Part of the multi-miner family alongside [[erga]], [[xena]], and [[zoya]], all built on [[honeycrisp]] primitives targeting zero-copy unified-memory dispatch across Apple Silicon coprocessors.
