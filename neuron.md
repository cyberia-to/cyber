the one who links. an agent with identity, stake, and will to shape the [[cybergraph]] — human, AI, sensor, or program. creates [[cyberlinks]], pays [[focus]], earns [[karma]]

two adjacent roles, not one noun:

- **signer** — the author of a [[signal]]. its clock is a [[step]] on its own chain (`prev`).
- **network** — the destination the signal is for (a card / named graph). its clock is a [[block]].

a neuron may sign into several networks; a network is not the same as the key that writes to it.

A robot attaches neurons for its selected keys, networks and devices. One neuron
can execute several [[progs]] with durable state and bounded invocations. Program,
task and checkpoint particles identify its work; the neuron supplies authorship.
Retiring a program preserves that subject identity.

The supported native key profile names the subject by H(compressed public key).
Foreign accounts retain their domain, address and network qualification. Current
host grants bind each action to its captured subject and destination. See the
[identity contract](../neuron/specs/identity.md) and
[service, book and shard roles](specs/domain-ladder.md).
