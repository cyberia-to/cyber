# learning incentives

every reward in [[cyber]] traces to one quantity: the [[impulse]] — the proven change in collective [[focus]] $\Delta\phi^*$ a [[neuron]] delivers by adding [[cyberlinks]]. a neuron computes the shift locally on its neighborhood, proves it with a [[zheng]] proof against the [[BBG]] root, and self-mints [[$CYB]] proportional to its fair share of the shift. minting is bounded by the actual global $\Delta\phi^*$ (conservation), so inflation is evidence of [[knowledge]] creation — there is no emission without demonstrated contribution. creating valuable structure is creating [[value]]: no designed loss function, the physics of [[convergence]] defines what deserves reward.

the full specification — reward functions, [[Shapley]] attribution, self-minting, token operations, and the settled design — lives in [[reward specification|tru/specs/rewards]]. see [[cyber/tokenomics]] for system-level economics and [[collective learning]] for group dynamics.
