# roadmap

the roadmap of [[cyber]] is the launch, in phases. the contract, the gates, the property registry and the calendar live on one page, [[cyber/launch]]; the engineering order under it lives in the [delivery roadmap](/cyber/roadmap/index). this page is the map between them and the record of what the previous roadmap became.

## now · phase 1 · 2026-11-05

the bootloader reborn: [[bootloader/bostrom|bostrom]] and [[bootloader/tokens/$PUSSY|pussy]] return as two [[soft3]] chains on the fifth anniversary of the bostrom genesis, minutes apart. four cores, none negotiable: mint by Shapley with settlement mining, fold, consensus by convergence in [[foculus]], and privacy. content that people linked must be visible in [[cyb]]: media render, a PDF reader, every referenced particle resolving. user programmability, UniversalHash, fees and [[$CYB]] wait.

freezes: core specs 2026-10-09 · features 2026-10-23 · genesis candidates 2026-10-28 · public rehearsal 2026-10-30 → 11-02.

## next · phase 2 · canary

ninety days of the two chains under real use with a weekly red team. upgrades in order: fold if the plan B was taken, then programmability ([B computation](roadmap/b-computation.md): nox programs, [[joy]], [[trident]] contracts), then the fee role. the parameter change procedure is exercised on pussy before bostrom needs it.

## then · phase 3 · $CYB mainnet

implementations in several languages agreeing on state roots, the formal spine, the six mainnet questions, the [[cybics/crystal|crystal]] as the genesis basis. the details stay on [[cyber/launch]].
