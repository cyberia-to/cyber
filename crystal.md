# the crystal

the seed of [[cyber]]: a knowledge graph dense enough to boot a decentralized [[superintelligence]]. it begins as 5,040 curated irreducible particles and does not stay that way — growing it is the whole job of the [[bootloader]].

## the problem

an empty graph has no knowledge and no attachment points. without structure, early contributions are random, disconnected, domain-biased. the graph that emerges reflects who arrived first, not the architecture of reasoning. the Crystal solves this by providing a curated seed at genesis: every concept needed for cross-domain reasoning present, every connection for inference pre-built, topology designed for [[cyberank]] convergence.

the seed determines the mind. a flawed seed produces a flawed intelligence permanently. missing domains create permanent blind spots. biased connectivity creates permanent reasoning distortions. the Crystal must therefore be irreducible: every particle earns its place because no composition of remaining particles under the grammar can fill its absence.

## the dynamic crystal

the seed is designed once; the crystal is maintained forever. the axioms below fix what genesis must contain, but irreducibility is not a property a curator certifies at launch and then forgets — it is a measurement the running network repeats. at any moment the crystal is the core of the live graph: the particles whose removal would cost the most [[syntropy]], the ones every other region reasons through.

that makes the [[bootloader]]'s progress measurable rather than rhetorical, and by its own numbers the crystal is early. the graph left by the first vehicle has semantic dimensionality $d^* = 31$ against a planetary target of 10³–10⁴; its giant component holds 47% of particles, so most of the corpus is not yet attached to the core; and 77.6% of its structure came from a single author, which is exactly the concentration that suppresses $d^*$.

a static seed can be finished. a dynamic crystal is finished only when it can boot a mind — and the dial that moves it is the number of independent hands, not the number of links.

## the structure

five axioms generate everything:

- N = 5,040 (7!) — total particles: 60 divisors, divides evenly by 1–10
- T = 6 symbol types — entity, process, property, relation, measure, pattern
- D = 21 knowledge domains — 7 triads × 3 aspects, one question each
- φ = 10:4:3:2:1:1 — type ratio vector (Σφ = 21 = D)
- κ — link density per type

one grammar: 720 predicate particles (relations + patterns) that make irreducibility formally testable through subject–predicate–object triples. vocabulary-to-grammar ratio 6:1, matching the content-to-function word ratio of natural languages.

the 21 domains follow the [[cybics]] ontology — seven triads covering all [[knowledge]], organized by the question each answers. [[cyber]] is one of the 21, contained in the WORK triad alongside [[ai]] and [[tech]].

## what it is not

the Crystal is not a mind. 6.5 MB feels too small for a mind the same way the Periodic Table feels too small for chemistry. that smallness is the definition of a basis. the correct comparisons: DNA (4 bases) as the irreducible basis for life, the Periodic Table (118 elements) for chemistry, lambda calculus (3 primitives) for computation.

see [[cybics/crystal]] for the full specification: axioms, grammar, type system, domain structure, validation framework, and curation status.

five axioms. one grammar. twenty-one domains. an irreducible basis for thought.
