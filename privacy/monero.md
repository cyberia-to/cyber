the chain that made privacy the default (2014, from the CryptoNote lineage). every transaction hides sender among decoys (ring signatures), hides amount (RingCT — Pedersen commitments with range proofs), hides recipient (one-time stealth addresses). nothing to opt into, nothing to leak by laziness

the lesson [[privacy]] inherits: an anonymity set is a commons — if privacy is optional, the cautious few stand out precisely by using it. monero's fungibility survives because everyone is in the crowd by construction
