sealing is the block height at which a [[signal]] is finalized — when the [[zheng]] proof is produced and the signal becomes irrevocable

sealing is the second timestamp of a two-part duration. together with [[inception]], it defines the span of a [[signal]]:

```
span = sealing_height − inception_height
```

at sealing, the [[neuron]] produces a [[zheng]] proof covering all [[cyberlinks]], the [[cyber/impulse]], and conviction box movements. this proof anchors the entire computation to the sealing height and the current [[BBG]] root

before sealing is assigned, the declared action exists as an [[intent]]. the act of sealing transforms the intent into a completed [[signal]]

an [[intent]] that never receives a sealing height becomes abandoned — on record at its [[inception]] height, permanently unfinished
