the promise token of [[cyber]]: hold tocyb, receive [[$CYB]] when the network arrives

# story

born in the [[bostrom]] genesis on november 5, 2021, alongside [[$BOOT]]. the early papers called it $C — carbon — but the denom on chain always read `tocyb`, and the name that stuck is the honest one: to cyb

the original design fixed the far cap at 1,000,000,000,000,000 units and promised 1 to 1 conversion into $CYB. 30% entered the bostrom genesis with the same structure as $BOOT — [[cybergift]] 70%, [[cybercongress]] 11.6%, [[epizode zero]] community 8.3%, [[senate]] 5.1%, [[great web foundation]] 5% — and 70% stayed unallocated, mass reserved for a network that was still a promise

through five years of the bootloader most of the allocation stayed parked in multisigs (the ledger of that era lives in [[finalization of $BOOT distribution]]); what was actually minted and circulating when the chain halted at height 25,120,712 on august 5, 2026 is the number that matters:

    281,405,532,467,645 tocyb — bank supply at halt

# resolution

the promise resolves in [[soft3]]. at [[$CYB]] genesis every tocyb becomes 666 CYB:

    281,405,532,467,645 × 666 = 187,416,084,623,451,570 CYB ≈ 1% of the field cap p

continuity of prior belief into the proof field — the 1:1 promise into a 1e15 cap became ×666 into the [[nebu|Goldilocks field]] cap, the same one percent of the money that was always meant for the believers. check any address, name or ledger at [snapshot.bostrom.network](https://bostrom.network) — the checker shows genesis CYB per holder
