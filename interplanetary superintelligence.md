[[superintelligence]] that spans planets — one [[cybergraph]] of [[neurons]] and [[particles]] across light-minutes of separation. Type I is a thinking planet. Type II is a thinking solar system. the energy and matter of many worlds only become a single mind if agreement survives the speed of light

## the latency problem

Earth to Mars is roughly 4–24 minutes one way. a round trip is often longer than a human conversation and longer than almost every classical [[consensus]] round designed for a data center or a planet

protocols that collect votes, elect leaders, or wait for two-thirds of a global committee must close a loop across space. every such round pays light-time twice: outbound and return. at interplanetary distance the committee freezes. the medium is not slow software — the medium is physics. any design whose liveness assumes sub-second or even sub-minute global synchrony is Earth-only by construction

the problem to name precisely: shared truth under asymmetric, multi-minute delay and long partitions, with each world living on its own facts while shared facts eventually agree

## the bar: foculus-class consensus

[[foculus]] is consensus by convergence. a [[particle]] is final when φ*_i > τ — when enough collective [[focus]] has gathered — an information condition, not a clock. nodes [[gossip]] signals and each runs the same [[tri-kernel]] over what it holds; the operator is a contraction, so identical signals yield one fixed point everywhere. no vote to collect, no leader to wait for, no round-trip to close agreement

that is why interplanetary aggregation is possible:

- agreement costs computation and local connectivity; propagation alone pays light-time
- each planet is its own domain pond — Martian chatter finalizes at Martian speed; Earth at Earth speed
- a dark link (conjunction, outage) stops cross-world trade of rings; it does not invent conflicting finals — safety holds under partial synchrony; liveness resumes when rings resume
- finality is “enough has gathered,” not “N minutes of global block time” — a stopwatch is meaningless when the message itself is 40 minutes old

voting BFT and Nakamoto-style global stopwatches are Earth-scale tools. interplanetary superintelligence requires a foculus-like mechanism: leaderless, information-final, domain-local, partition-safe. that is how high the bar is — not a feature checklist, a physics bound on what can count as one mind across worlds
