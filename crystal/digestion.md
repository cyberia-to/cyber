# digestion

The process of breaking down food into absorbable nutrients through mechanical and enzymatic action.

Begins in the mouth with chewing and salivary amylase acting on [[starch]], continues through the stomach and intestines.

Gastric acid and proteases denature proteins, while bile salts emulsify fats for lipase activity.

[[Dietary fiber]] regulates transit time and supports a healthy gut [[microbiome]].

Nutrient absorption occurs primarily in the small intestine through specialized epithelial cells.

The large intestine ferments residual [[fiber]], producing short-chain fatty acids essential for colon health.
