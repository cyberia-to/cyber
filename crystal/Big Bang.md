origin of the observable universe ~13.8 billion years ago

initial state of extreme density and temperature, expanding and cooling ever since

first seconds: quarks, leptons, photons; first minutes: hydrogen, helium nucleosynthesis

cosmic microwave background radiation: snapshot of the universe at 380,000 years

expansion governed by [[gravity]], dark matter, dark [[energy]]

stars formed from hydrogen clouds, nucleosynthesis forged heavier elements up to [[iron]]

galaxies, clusters, cosmic web: large-scale structure emerged from quantum fluctuations

the arrow of time and [[entropy]] originate here

link to [[cosmology]]: the study of the universe's origin, structure, and fate

all [[geological time]], all biology, all civilization trace back to this singular event
