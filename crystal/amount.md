# amount

A quantity or measure of substance.

In recipes, the amount specifies the weight or volume of an ingredient required for preparation.
Common units include grams, milliliters, cups, tablespoons, and pieces.
Precise amounts ensure reproducibility and consistency across culinary experiments.
In the [[cyber]] protocol, amount refers to the numeric value of a [[token]] transfer between accounts.
On-chain amounts are denominated in the smallest indivisible unit of the token.
The concept bridges physical measurement and digital accounting within the [[cybergraph]].
