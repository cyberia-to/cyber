Sign is a product within the [[cyber]] ecosystem for cryptographic signing of transactions and messages. Digital signatures form the authentication layer that enables trustless interaction across decentralized networks.

Every action on the [[cyber]] protocol requires a valid cryptographic signature from the sender's private key. Sign provides the interface through which users authorize [[cyberlinks]], token transfers, governance votes, and other on-chain operations.

The signing process transforms a user's intent into a mathematically verifiable proof of authorship. This proof travels with the transaction through the network, allowing any validator to confirm its authenticity without relying on a central authority.

Sign integrates with [[cyb]] and other interfaces in the ecosystem, offering a consistent signing experience whether the user operates from a browser extension, mobile wallet, or hardware device.

Secure key management underlies the entire signing workflow. Users maintain sovereignty over their private keys, ensuring that the ability to sign remains exclusively under their control.
