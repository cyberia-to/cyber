distance between consecutive peaks (or troughs) of a [[wave]]

measured in meters; light wavelengths typically in nanometers (nm)

determines [[color]] in the visible spectrum: ~380 nm (violet) to ~700 nm (red)

determines pitch in [[sound]]: longer wavelength = lower frequency = deeper tone

related to [[frequency]] by: wavelength = speed / frequency

electromagnetic spectrum spans radio waves (km) to gamma rays (pm)

de Broglie wavelength assigns a wavelength to particles with [[momentum]]

diffraction and interference effects depend on the ratio of wavelength to obstacle size

antenna length scales with the wavelength of the signal it transmits or receives
