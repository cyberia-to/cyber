# avocado oil

A cooking oil pressed from the fruit of [[avocado]]. High smoke point (271 C) makes it ideal for high-heat cooking.

Rich in oleic acid and [[vitamin]] E, avocado oil supports cardiovascular health and skin nourishment.

The mild, buttery flavor complements salads, grilling, and roasting without overpowering other ingredients.

Cold-pressed varieties retain the highest concentration of beneficial [[chemical compounds]].

Avocado oil also serves as a base in [[massage oil]] and cosmetic formulations.
