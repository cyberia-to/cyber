[[plant]]s used for flavoring, [[medicine]], or fragrance

herbs are typically the leafy green or [[flower]]ing parts of a [[plant]], distinguished from [[spice]]s which derive from [[seed]]s, [[bark]], or [[root]]s

culinary herbs include [[basil]], [[rosemary]], [[thyme]], and [[parsley]], while medicinal herbs include [[chamomile]], [[echinacea]], and [[valerian]]

herbal knowledge forms the foundation of [[traditional medicine]] systems worldwide, and many herbs contain [[antimicrobial]], [[anti-inflammatory]], and [[antioxidant]] compounds
