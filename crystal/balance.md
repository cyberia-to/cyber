# balance

A state of equilibrium between opposing forces, where inputs and outputs reach a stable configuration.

In [[economics]], balance emerges when supply meets demand at a clearing price. In [[ecology]], ecosystem balance arises from the interplay of [[species]] populations, nutrient cycles, and energy flows. In protocol design, stake-weighted balance of [[focus]] ensures that network resources align with participant commitment.

Physical balance governs [[biomechanics]] and movement coordination. Chemical balance drives [[reaction]] equilibria. Informational balance shapes [[consensus]] mechanisms.

Systems that maintain balance through feedback loops exhibit [[resilience]]. Systems that lose balance cascade toward phase transitions.
