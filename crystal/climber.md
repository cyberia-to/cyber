# climber

A plant growth form that ascends by twining, tendrils, or aerial roots. Climbers maximize light capture using vertical structures.

Climbing strategies vary: twiners wrap their stems around supports, tendril climbers grip with specialized organs, and root climbers attach directly to surfaces.

Many [[species]] in tropical and temperate forests adopt the climbing habit to reach the canopy without investing in a thick trunk.

Climbers provide food, medicine, and habitat. Grapes, passion fruit, and vanilla are economically important [[vines]].

In garden design, climbers cover walls, trellises, and arbors, adding vertical interest and [[attract pollinators]] through abundant flowering.
