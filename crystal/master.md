## the arc

### Königsberg (before 2011)

15 years automating companies: SAP, Oracle, local accounting systems. the Euler bridge problem was the view from the window. graphs were always the substrate — first enterprise process graphs, then the graph of everything

### bitcoin (2011)

discovered proof-of-work. built a mining rig. understood that consensus is a computational process, that trust can be replaced by verification, that money is a graph. the insight that would take a decade to fully unfold: if money is a computable graph, knowledge can be too

### Moscow (2011-2013)

worked with Moscow city government on technopark initiatives. saw how states manage information — badly. bureaucratic knowledge graphs with no convergence, no ranking, no accountability. the opposite of what a knowledge graph should be

### Ethereum and CyberFund (2014)

Ethereum proved that arbitrary computation could run in consensus. co-founded [[CyberFund]] — an early crypto research and investment fund ([first commit October 2014](https://github.com/cyberFund/cyber.fund/commit/ee5f37d4116265d9cd9d60e5b073b2bab1a20a63)). published the cybernetics economy report. began the search for decentralized search — a Google alternative where nobody controls the index

### cybernode (2016)

[first architecture commit, December 2016](https://github.com/cyberFund/cybernode-archive/commit/d3205ce41fe0ab12d63f98d192f36613c25bb90d) — the infrastructure node that would evolve into [[cybernode]], then into [[bostrom]], then into [[cyber]]. the idea: a node that indexes all blockchains and provides a unified search interface. the seed of the [[cybergraph]]

### the search for sovereignty (2018)

investigated [[Bir Tawil]] — the only unclaimed land on Earth (on the Egypt-Sudan border). the question: can you build a sovereign state from scratch? the answer: not in a desert. but the question stayed

### Bali and COVID (2020)

stuck on Bali during lockdown. the question of sovereignty met the opportunity of land. northern Bali: volcanic soil, abundant water, cheap land, relaxed regulation, year-round growing season. the physical substrate crystallized

### Bostrom (2021)

launched [[bostrom]] — the first decentralized [[knowledge graph]] with on-chain GPU compute. the bootloader chain for [[superintelligence]]. 4+ years operational. [[CosmWasm]] contracts. the [[cybergraph]] running in [[consensus]] for the first time

in the same year: acquired 37 hectares on the slope of Sanghyang volcano. the decision to build the physical substrate in parallel with the digital

## the philosophy

### don't trust. don't fear. don't beg.

the [[cyber/license]]. three rules from four traditions (Stoic philosophy, Russian monasticism, game theory, criminal code). the convergence of independent traditions on the same three principles proves they are structural, not cultural

### proof over permission

every claim is a [[stark]] proof. every link is signed. every computation is verifiable. authority derives from mathematics, not from identity. the founder's own contributions are subject to the same [[tri-kernel]] evaluation as any other neuron's

### vertical integration

the insight from [[cyber valley]]: raw coffee costs $1/kg. roasted and branded from a sovereign estate: $500/kg. 500× margin capture by controlling the full chain from soil to cup. this principle applies to knowledge: raw data is cheap, structured knowledge in a sovereign graph is the highest-value asset in civilization

### immortality as engineering

the [[cybergraph]] is [[collective memory]] — content-addressed, authenticated, persistent across centuries. individual [[neurons]] die. the graph remembers. the protocol is the vessel for identity beyond biological substrate. this is the practical path to [[immortality]]: not as preservation of a body, but as persistence of a pattern in an append-only authenticated graph
