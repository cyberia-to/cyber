# type

Classification of plants by growth form, describing the primary structural category of a [[species]].

Growth forms include [[tree]], [[shrub]], [[climber]], [[grass]], [[herb]], [[fern]], and [[palm]].

Each growth form occupies a distinct vertical layer in [[food forest]] and [[permaculture]] systems.

Species pages reference a single primary type to enable filtering and layer assignment in the [[cyber valley]] species database.

Understanding growth form helps predict canopy height, root depth, light requirements, and companion planting compatibility.
