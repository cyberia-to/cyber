# muscle

Contractile tissue in animals that enables movement through coordinated fiber contraction.

[[Skeletal muscle]] attaches to [[bones]] via [[tendons]], allowing voluntary movement and posture control. [[Smooth muscle]] lines internal [[organs]] like the stomach and blood vessels, operating involuntarily. [[Cardiac muscle]] drives the [[heart]] with rhythmic, self-sustaining contractions.

Muscle tissue converts chemical energy from [[ATP]] into mechanical force. Training and [[nutrition]] determine muscle adaptation, growth, and endurance capacity.

All three muscle types share the fundamental property of excitability, contractility, extensibility, and elasticity.
