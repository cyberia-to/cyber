Salvia rosmarinus, an [[aromatic]] evergreen shrub of the [[Lamiaceae]] family with [[antimicrobial]] and [[anti-inflammatory]] properties

rosemary contains [[rosmarinic acid]], [[cineole]], [[camphor]], and [[carnosic acid]], contributing to its [[antioxidant]] and neuroprotective activity

used in [[culinary]] traditions across the Mediterranean, rosemary also serves as a memory-enhancing and circulation-stimulating [[herb]] in [[traditional medicine]]

rosemary thrives in well-drained [[soil]] and full sun, making it a resilient [[garden]] [[plant]]
