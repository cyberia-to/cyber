wavelength:: 495-570 nm

emotion:: [[joy]]

the center of the visible spectrum. peak of human visual sensitivity

evolutionary signal: vegetation, [[photosynthesis]], fertility, safety, abundance

## properties

- the eye has more green-sensitive cones than any other — maximum discrimination in this range
- lowest visual fatigue — the most restful color
- the color of [[chlorophyll]], the molecule that converts sunlight to life

## in nature

- [[photosynthesis]]: the process that makes Earth habitable. green is the color of life itself
- healthy vegetation: dense green canopy signals water, nutrients, food, shelter
- camouflage: the baseline of the natural world, the color everything hides in
- growth: shoots, sprouts, new leaves — green is the color of becoming

## in [[prysm]]

- confidence, success, health, growth, confirmation
- the default positive state — what [[cyb]] feels like when everything works

## the deepest binding

- [[chlorophyll]] absorbs red and blue, reflects green — the color of life is the wavelength life rejects
- [[photosynthesis]] converts solar energy into chemical energy. [[computation]] converts solar energy into knowledge
- the [[isomorphism]] between these two processes makes green the natural color of intelligence
