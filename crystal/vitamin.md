organic compounds required in small quantities for metabolic function. vitamins cannot be synthesized in sufficient amounts by the body and must come from diet — primarily from [[fruit]], [[vegetables]], and [[herbs]]

fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) accumulate in tissue. water-soluble vitamins (B complex, C) require regular intake. deficiency produces specific pathologies: scurvy (C), rickets (D), beriberi (B1), pellagra (B3)

many [[species]] in the [[cyber valley]] catalog are documented for their vitamin content. [[moringa oleifera]] contains vitamins A, C, and E. tropical [[fruit]] provides vitamin C in abundance. fermented foods increase B vitamin bioavailability
