circular component rotating on an axle

one of six simple machines, alongside [[lever]], wedge, screw, pulley, inclined plane

invented circa 3500 BCE in Mesopotamia, first for pottery then transport

applications: vehicles, gears, turbines, flywheels, [[pump]] impellers

converts linear force to rotational motion and back

foundational to nearly all mechanical [[energy]] transmission

the [[engine]] is a wheel-dependent system: crankshaft, camshaft, drivetrain

scaling from nanometer gears to wind turbine rotors
