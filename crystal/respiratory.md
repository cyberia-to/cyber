conditions affecting the airways and lungs — from acute infections (pneumonia, bronchitis, [[influenza virus]]) to chronic diseases (asthma, COPD). respiratory infections remain a leading cause of mortality globally

plant-derived compounds address respiratory conditions through multiple mechanisms. [[cineole]] (from eucalyptus and [[rosemary]]) acts as a bronchodilator and mucolytic. [[anti-inflammatory]] compounds reduce airway inflammation. [[antimicrobial]] agents from [[garlic]], thyme, and ginger target respiratory pathogens

[[traditional medicine]] systems use inhalation of [[essential oils]], herbal teas, and steam preparations for respiratory relief. many of these practices have been validated by modern pharmacology
