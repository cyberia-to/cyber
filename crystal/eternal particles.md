# eternal particles

[[Particles]] with permanently anchored [[pi-weight]] via [[CYB]] burn. The burned amount sets a floor weight that cannot be archived, deprioritized, or decayed by any mechanism.

Eternal particles represent the graph's long-term assertions — facts, definitions, and core knowledge that their creators deem worth preserving indefinitely.

Creating an eternal particle requires irreversible destruction of [[CYB]], making the commitment costly and deliberate. This economic friction filters for high-conviction knowledge.

The permanence guarantee means [[cybergraph]] retains a stable semantic backbone even as transient particles cycle through archival and expiration.
