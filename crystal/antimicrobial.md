a property of substances that kill or inhibit the growth of [[microorganism]]s including [[bacteria]], [[virus]]es, [[fungi]], and [[parasite]]s

[[antimicrobial]] agents are central to [[medicine]], [[food]] preservation, and [[hygiene]]

subclasses include [[antibacterial]], [[antifungal]], and [[antiviral]] activity

many [[plant]]s and [[essential oil]]s exhibit natural antimicrobial properties through [[secondary metabolite]]s such as [[terpene]]s, [[phenol]]s, and [[alkaloid]]s
