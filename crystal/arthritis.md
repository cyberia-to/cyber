chronic [[inflammation]] of joints causing pain, swelling, and reduced range of motion

major forms include osteoarthritis (wear of [[cartilage]]) and rheumatoid arthritis (an [[autoimmune]] disorder)

management involves [[anti-inflammatory]] agents, [[exercise]], and dietary support

many [[plant]]-derived [[compound]]s such as [[curcumin]], [[boswellic acid]], and [[ginger]] extracts show therapeutic potential
