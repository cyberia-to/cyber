# ecosystem

A community of interacting organisms and their physical environment functioning as a system. Energy flows and nutrient cycles link biotic and abiotic components into a dynamic whole.

[[Producers]] capture solar energy through photosynthesis, [[consumers]] transfer it through trophic levels, and [[decomposers]] recycle matter back into the soil and atmosphere.

Ecosystems range from a puddle to the entire [[biosphere]]. Each has characteristic [[species]] composition, energy budgets, and disturbance regimes that shape its trajectory over time.

Resilience depends on [[biodiversity]] — diverse ecosystems absorb perturbations and recover faster than simplified ones.
