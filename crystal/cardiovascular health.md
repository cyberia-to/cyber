the functional state of the [[heart]] and blood vessels, reflecting their capacity to circulate blood efficiently

cardiovascular health depends on arterial elasticity, blood pressure regulation, lipid balance, and endothelial integrity

[[diet]], [[exercise]], and [[stress]] management are primary determinants, with [[omega-3]] fatty acids, [[garlic]], and [[flavonoid]]s offering protective effects

impaired cardiovascular health leads to conditions such as [[hypertension]], [[atherosclerosis]], and [[stroke]]
