a [[neuron]] reads what the [[tru]] computed — [[cyberank]], [[karma]] — and decides what to [[link]] next. the moment between [[inference]] and [[learning]], where [[feedback]] closes the loop

observation is the sensory act of the [[cyber]] protocol. the [[tru]] publishes the current [[state]] — rankings, token balances, graph topology — and every [[neuron]] can query it. what a neuron observes determines what it will [[link]], [[stake]], or [[delegate]] in the next [[round]]

the observation surface includes [[cyberank]] scores (which [[particles]] the collective [[attention]] favors), [[karma]] (the neuron's own accumulated reputation), and the full [[cybergraph]] structure (who linked what to what). a neuron can also observe [[signals]] from other neurons, building a local model of the network's intentions

observation creates [[feedback]]: a neuron sees the effect of its previous [[cyberlinks]] reflected in updated rankings, then adjusts its strategy. this observe-act-observe cycle is the heartbeat of [[cybernetics]] inside the protocol

for autonomous agents ([[souls]]), observation is programmatic — a smart contract reads on-chain [[state]] and triggers conditional [[signals]]. for human [[neurons]], observation happens through the [[cyb]] interface, where [[search]] results and [[feed]] rankings present the current cyberank surface

the quality of observation determines the quality of [[learning]]. a neuron that observes broadly builds a richer model of the [[cybergraph]] and produces more effective [[cyberlinks]]
