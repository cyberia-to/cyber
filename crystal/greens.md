# greens

Leafy green vegetables harvested for their edible [[leaves]], consumed raw or cooked across global cuisines.

Rich in vitamins, minerals, and [[chlorophyll]], [[greens]] supply essential micronutrients with minimal caloric density.

Common varieties include spinach, kale, chard, arugula, and lettuce, each with distinct flavor and nutritional profiles.

[[Greens]] thrive in cool seasons and partial shade, making them ideal for year-round container and [[succession]] planting.

Fresh [[greens]] form the foundation of salads, smoothies, and fermented preparations.

In [[permaculture]] gardens, leafy [[greens]] serve as fast-cycling ground cover between slower-growing [[trees]] and [[shrubs]].
