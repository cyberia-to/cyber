the process of change in heritable characteristics of [[population]]s over successive generations

driven by [[natural selection]], [[mutation]], [[genetic drift]], and [[gene flow]]

the mechanism by which biological complexity arises: from single-celled [[organism]]s to the diversity of [[life]] on [[earth]]

[[Charles Darwin]] and [[Alfred Russel Wallace]] independently formulated the theory of evolution by natural selection

foundational to [[biology]], [[genetics]], and understanding [[adaptation]]
