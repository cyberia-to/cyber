the primary photosynthetic organ of a [[plant]]. leaves capture light energy, exchange gases (CO2 in, O2 out), and transpire water — collectively driving the carbon cycle and atmospheric composition

leaf morphology varies enormously: simple vs compound, alternate vs opposite, entire vs serrated margins. these traits are key identifiers in [[species]] classification and are documented in the plant/features section of each species page

many medicinal and culinary applications use leaves specifically. [[herbs]] are defined by leaf use. [[tea]], [[moringa oleifera]] powder, and leafy [[vegetables]] deliver concentrated [[phytochemicals]] through leaf tissue
