Mangifera indica, a tropical [[fruit]] tree of the [[Anacardiaceae]] family cultivated for its sweet [[fruit]]

mango fruit provides [[vitamin C]], [[vitamin A]], [[fiber]], and [[polyphenol]]s including [[mangiferin]], contributing to [[antioxidant]] and [[anti-inflammatory]] activity

the tree is native to South Asia and thrives in tropical and subtropical climates, producing hundreds of cultivar varieties

mango holds cultural significance across India, Southeast Asia, and Latin America, serving as both a fresh [[staple]] and a source of dried, pickled, and juiced products
