output routed back as input — the foundation of [[cybernetics]]. in [[cyber]], the [[neuron]] observes [[cyberank]], adjusts [[cyberlinks]], and the [[cybergraph]] shifts beneath everyone's feet

[[norbert wiener]] defined [[cybernetics]] as the study of control through feedback. the [[cyber]] protocol embeds this principle at the architectural level: every [[signal]] a [[neuron]] sends alters the [[cybergraph]], which alters [[cyberank]], which alters what the neuron [[observation|observes]], which alters the next signal

feedback in [[cyber]] operates across multiple timescales. within a single [[round]], the [[tru]] recomputes [[cyberank]] and publishes updated rankings. across rounds, [[neurons]] observe shifts in [[karma]] and adjust their [[cyberlinks]] strategy. over epochs, the cumulative feedback shapes which [[particles]] rise to prominence and which fade

positive feedback amplifies: when many [[neurons]] link to the same [[particle]], its [[cyberank]] rises, making it more visible in [[search]], attracting more links. this is how [[consensus]] around valuable knowledge emerges organically

negative feedback stabilizes: a [[neuron]] that wastes [[focus]] on low-value [[cyberlinks]] sees its [[karma]] stagnate relative to peers, incentivizing course correction. the [[stake]]-weighted nature of the ranking means economic skin in the game disciplines the loop

for [[souls]] (autonomous programs), feedback is the control signal that closes the [[intelligence]] loop — the soul observes, computes, acts, and observes again, converging toward whatever objective its logic encodes
