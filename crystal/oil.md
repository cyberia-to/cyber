a hydrophobic liquid extracted from [[plant]]s or animals, used in cooking, [[medicine]], and industry

plant-derived oils include [[olive oil]], [[coconut oil]], and [[sesame oil]], obtained from [[seed]]s, [[fruit]]s, or [[nut]]s through pressing or distillation

essential oils are volatile [[aromatic]] compounds concentrated from [[herbs]], [[flower]]s, and [[bark]], valued for [[antimicrobial]], [[anti-inflammatory]], and therapeutic properties

oils serve as carriers for fat-soluble [[vitamin]]s and provide [[fatty acid]]s essential to [[metabolism]]
