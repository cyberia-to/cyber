an RNA [[virus]] of the family Orthomyxoviridae causing respiratory infection in humans and animals

influenza virus spreads through airborne droplets and contact with contaminated surfaces, producing [[fever]], cough, and muscle pain

three types circulate in humans: influenza A (the source of pandemics), influenza B, and influenza C

the virus undergoes rapid [[mutation]] through antigenic drift and shift, requiring annual vaccine reformulation

many [[plant]]s and [[essential oil]]s exhibit [[antiviral]] activity against influenza, including [[elderberry]], [[garlic]], and [[eucalyptus]]
