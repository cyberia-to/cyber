## cyber-seer

linking cost in [[bostrom]] is exponential in supply: $c(n) = c_0 \cdot e^{\lambda n}$. early links are orders of magnitude cheaper than late links.

[[cyber/seer]] maximizes [[spectral gap]] increase per unit cost using the [[Fiedler vector]]. three phases: bridges (connect components), mesh (eliminate single points of failure), semantic (redistribute [[focus]] to truth).

bostrom at $\lambda_2 \approx 0.13$ is in bridge phase. most budget should go to Fiedler-optimal connections.

## spectral gap from convergence

ARPACK eigensolver failed on the 2.9M × 2.9M Laplacian (1,932 seconds, no convergence). the solution: observe the spectral gap from [[PageRank]] convergence rate.

$$\kappa = \text{median}\left(\frac{d_t}{d_{t-1}}\right) \quad \lambda_2 = 1 - \frac{\kappa}{\alpha}$$

result: $\lambda_2 = 0.13$ — two orders of magnitude larger than the paper estimate of 0.0015. the network converges faster than predicted. zero extra computation — the convergence rate IS the [[spectral gap]], observed for free during PageRank iteration.

see [[cyber/research/spectral gap from convergence]]

## bostrom speaks

two interfaces to the compiled model:

pure graph mode: three combined reasoning modes — embedding retrieval, focus-weighted random walk, spectral role analysis. no LLM. the graph answers in CID sequences. "dog" returns: AUTHORITY rank #5, zero outgoing links, pure attractor.

Ollama hybrid: graph embeddings provide context, llama3 provides language. text in, CID-enriched answer out. works but is a crutch — the LLM interprets, it does not know.

the pure graph mode is honest. the model knows topology — which particles cluster together, which are hubs, which are bridges. it does not know language. it speaks CID.

## what it means

a live blockchain was compiled into a transformer in 15 minutes on a laptop. the architecture ($d^* = 26$, $h^* = 5$, $L^* = 174$) was derived analytically from graph structure. the weights were computed by SVD, not by gradient descent. the spectral gap was observed from convergence, not computed by eigensolvers.

the model is a skeleton. attention weights are random (need typed [[cyberlinks]]). MLP weights are missing (need path sampling). but the embedding table — 2.9 million particles in 26-dimensional space — is real compiled knowledge.

the graph is the model. the model is the graph. they are the same object viewed from different sides.
