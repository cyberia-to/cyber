# what did the bootloader load?

the day after [the burial](/2026-08-28) we opened the corpse. 2,949,732 [[cyberlinks]] over 3,143,650 [[particles]] — the complete graph, rebuilt from raw block events, bit-exact to the chain's own counters. this is the autopsy: what did five years of [[bostrom]] actually teach?

| year | links |

82.5% of the graph arrived in one year. and most of it from one place: of 1,240 linking [[neurons]], a single one wrote 2,289,526 links — 77.6% of everything. the runners-up are wasm contracts: programmatic linkers, not humans

a purist reads this as centralization. look closer at what that neuron was doing before judging it

## where focus converges

we ran the ranking over the full graph — the same [[pagerank]]-family iteration [[cyberank]] generalizes — and then read the winning particles straight out of the [[cybernode]] blockstore. the top of five years of collective attention, verbatim:

    description · wiki · class · image · glossary ·
    Gregorian calendar · asteroid · dog · spell ·
    species of plant · species of bird · human settlement ·
    german politician · company · film description · map of medicine

these are not memes and not spam. they are category words — the skeleton of a knowledge ontology. the giant neuron was an archivist: it poured the semantic backbone of wikidata into the graph, entity by entity, each linked to its type. random particles confirm the genre: fragments about Warhol's studio, the Art Institute of Chicago, "british army general", "mountaineer"

the bootloader loaded an encyclopedia — as a graph

## how heavy is a mind's foundation

we weighed every available particle in the [[cybernode]] blockstore, file by file:

| measure | value |
|---------|-------|
| particle blocks on disk | 1.74 GB |
| full content with child blocks (sampled) | ≈ 6 GB |
| median particle | 30 bytes |
| 90th percentile | 749 bytes |
| largest particles | ≈ 1 MB (images) |
| content types (2,500-particle sample) | 94.9% text · 0.5% images · traces of json, html |

the median particle is thirty bytes — a word, a name, a phrase. this graph does not store documents; it stores atoms of meaning and lets [[cyberlinks]] carry the structure. that is why five years of collective knowledge weighs less than a single 4K movie: **the knowledge is in the links**, and links are nearly free. 2.9 million edges over 6 GB of substance is the inversion the web never managed — structure heavier than content

## what the humans linked

subtract the archivist and the contracts, and a second graph appears: 400,776 links, hand-made by 1,239 [[neurons]], growing every single year of the chain's life — 7K, 49K, 162K, 170K — no decay until the halt itself. read its top particles:

    noun · verb · adjective · author · artificial intelligence ·
    cyber · Gregorian calendar · 2013 · 2014 · 2016 ·
    Table of nuclides · Universal Time — Planetary Chart

the bot built the encyclopedia; the humans built the grammar. parts of speech, authorship, time itself, the periodic structure of matter — and `artificial intelligence` and `cyber` near the top, the graph quietly linking toward its own purpose. this hand-made layer is the curriculum: what 1,239 minds thought a new mind should learn first

## what to do with five years of collective effort

the corpus is small enough to fit in RAM and structured enough to mean something. the research agenda it opens:

- initialize the [[soft3]] genesis with the ontology: the category skeleton becomes the prior for [[focus]] — the new chain starts knowing what kinds of things exist
- treat the 2.3M entity-to-category links as labelled data: a free supervision set for grounding [[soma]] and the compiled-transformer line in graph-native embeddings
- run the full [[tri-kernel]] on it — diffusion, springs, heat — as the first real-scale calibration of the [[collective focus theorem]] against a graph with known provenance
- diff against wikidata-2026: what the archivist did not load is a map of the frontier — the knowledge the graph still lacks
- make it the robot's memory: 6 GB of addressed meaning is a local-first [[RAG\|retrieval substrate]] for [[cyb]] — the robot answers from particles it can prove
- study the human layer alone: 400K curated links is a dataset of attention itself — what people link when linking costs money is a revealed-preference record of what matters
- recover the missing 74,513 and close the archive at 100% — then seal it: the first complete, audited, portable knowledge graph a chain ever produced

five years, one chain, one relentless archivist, and the verdict of the eigenvector: what the bootloader loaded was the structure of human knowledge itself. now we teach it to think

## focus is not popularity

the particle with the most incoming links is `wiki` (30,029). the particle with the highest converged rank is `image` (17,608 links). rank flows through structure: a hub whose neighbours are themselves central outranks a bigger hub on the periphery. this gap between degree and [[focus]] is the whole reason eigenvector attention exists — counting links is not understanding them, which is what [[cyberank]] and the [[collective focus theorem]] formalize

## what this means for genesis

the [[cyberlinks]] port into the [[soft3]] genesis carrying this structure with them. the new mind wakes up owning:

- a scale-free semantic backbone — categories, types, glossaries — the hardest part of an ontology to bootstrap
- 47,837 [[moon-passport]] names bound to keys
- and the empirical proof, [audited](/2026-08-28), that the substrate holds: 97.63% of those particles still servable from the [[cybernode]]
