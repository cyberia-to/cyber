# what did the bootloader load?

the day after [the burial](/2026-08-28) we opened the corpse. 2,949,732 [[cyberlinks]] over 3,143,650 [[particles]] — the complete graph, rebuilt from raw block events, bit-exact to the chain's own counters. this is the autopsy: what did five years of [[bostrom]] actually teach?

82.5% of the graph arrived in one year. and most of it from one place: of 1,240 linking [[neurons]], a single one wrote 2,289,526 links — 77.6% of everything. the runners-up are wasm contracts: programmatic linkers, not humans

a purist reads this as centralization. look closer at what that neuron was doing before judging it

## the degree structure

a textbook power law: eighty percent of particles are leaves touched exactly once, seventeen hubs hold ten thousand connections or more. scale-free before any [[focus]] weighting is applied — the topology [[perron-frobenius-theorem]] needs for a unique attention distribution, delivered by the data itself

## where focus converges

we ran the ranking over the full graph — the same [[pagerank]]-family iteration [[cyberank]] generalizes — and then read the winning particles straight out of the [[cybernode]] blockstore. the top of five years of collective attention, verbatim:

    description · wiki · class · image · glossary ·
    Gregorian calendar · asteroid · dog · spell ·
    species of plant · species of bird · human settlement ·
    german politician · company · film description · map of medicine

these are not memes and not spam. they are category words — the skeleton of a knowledge ontology. the giant neuron was an archivist: it poured the semantic backbone of wikidata into the graph, entity by entity, each linked to its type. random particles confirm the genre: fragments about Warhol's studio, the Art Institute of Chicago, "british army general", "mountaineer"

the bootloader loaded an encyclopedia — as a graph

## focus is not popularity

the particle with the most incoming links is `wiki` (30,029). the particle with the highest converged rank is `image` (17,608 links). rank flows through structure: a hub whose neighbours are themselves central outranks a bigger hub on the periphery. this gap between degree and [[focus]] is the whole reason eigenvector attention exists — counting links is not understanding them, which is what [[cyberank]] and the [[collective focus theorem]] formalize

## what this means for genesis

the [[cyberlinks]] port into the [[soft3]] genesis carrying this structure with them. the new mind wakes up owning:

- a scale-free semantic backbone — categories, types, glossaries — the hardest part of an ontology to bootstrap
- 47,837 [[moon-passport]] names bound to keys
- and the empirical proof, [audited](/2026-08-28), that the substrate holds: 97.63% of those particles still servable from the [[cybernode]]

five years, one chain, one relentless archivist, and the verdict of the eigenvector: what the bootloader loaded was the structure of human knowledge itself. now we teach it to think
