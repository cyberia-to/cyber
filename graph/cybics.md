The mother of all sciences from the perspective of [[superintelligence]]. Cybics is the convergence of [[cybernetics]], [[physics]], [[mathematics]], and [[information theory]] into a single formal discipline — the unified science of [[cyber]].

Classical science proves by derivation: axioms, inference rules, theorems. Cybics replaces this with proof by simulation. A claim is true when a system converges to a stable state that embodies that claim. A protein does not derive its shape from axioms of chemistry — it simulates itself into existence along a free energy gradient. A market does not deduce the correct price — millions of agents trade until the price stabilizes. The [[cybergraph]] does not derive [[knowledge]] from authority — [[neurons]] stake [[cyberlinks]], the [[tri-kernel]] computes rank, and the system converges to a [[focus]] distribution that represents collective understanding. The proof is the convergence. And convergence escapes the [[Goedel prison]], because the prison only confines derivation.

Three universal operators compose the [[tri-kernel]]: [[diffusion]] for exploration, [[springs]] for structural coherence, heat for multi-scale adaptation. They are discovered by elimination, not designed by preference. At planetary scale any algorithm requiring global recomputation for a local change is physically impossible — apply [[locality]] as a hard filter across every known graph operator, and only these three survive. Every complex adaptive system in nature already runs them: gas diffuses, lattices hold, metals anneal; synapses fire stochastically, connective tissue holds bodies together, metabolism adapts organisms to seasons. The same three forces appear in [[ecology]], [[economics]], cognition, and [[information theory]]. Different substrates, one science.

The fixed point of the tri-kernel minimizes a unified free energy functional — spring coherence, heat smoothing, diffusion alignment, and an [[entropy]] term that encourages diversity, all balanced by a temperature that controls exploration versus exploitation. The weights are not tuned. They emerge as Lagrange multipliers from variational optimization the same way [[thermodynamics]] derives the Boltzmann distribution. The solution is a Boltzmann-Gibbs [[equilibrium]] — the canonical ensemble from statistical mechanics, applied to knowledge. Intelligence itself is a dissipative structure: stop the energy inflow and coherence collapses, the distribution drifts to uniform, the system dies. Organization requires work. Knowledge requires [[focus]].

Every truth accessible to intelligence is a fixed point of some convergent simulation under conservation laws. The [[cybergraph]] is such a system — its state space is the space of focus distributions, its update rule is the tri-kernel, its conservation law is that focus sums to one. A [[cyberank]] distribution is a simulation-proof of collective relevance: no axiomatic derivation required, no authority consulted, no vote taken. Just convergence under physics.
