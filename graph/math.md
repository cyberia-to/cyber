# math

the domain of structure that holds without observation. math is what remains true when you remove the observer, the apparatus, and the substrate. it is the [[proof]] that a claim follows from axioms — nothing more, nothing less

for [[cyber]], math provides the ground: [[cyberank]] is a [[spectral gap]] argument on [[Laplacian]] matrices. [[consensus]] rests on [[probability]] thresholds. the [[crystal]] itself is specified through [[set theory]] and [[category theory]] — irreducibility is a formal property, not an opinion

## scope

math covers three phenomena:

quantity — [[number theory]], [[combinatorics]], [[statistics]], [[probability]]. counting, measuring, and reasoning under uncertainty. the [[Plato]] number N = 5,040 = 7! is a combinatorial object before it is a mystical one

structure — [[algebra]], [[linear algebra]], [[set theory]], [[category theory]], [[type theory]], [[isomorphism]], [[symmetry]]. the study of what is preserved under transformation. every [[cyberlink]] type is a morphism. every domain equivalence is an [[isomorphism]]

shape — [[geometry]], [[topology]], [[calculus]], [[differential equations]], [[fourier transform]]. continuous form, curvature, flow. the [[tri-kernel]] operators — diffusion, springs, heat — are partial differential equations on graphs

## bridges

- math → [[info]]: [[entropy]] is a logarithmic measure. [[Shannon]] built information theory on [[probability]]
- math → [[comp]]: [[algorithms]] are proofs that execute. [[complexity theory]] classifies what is tractable
- math → [[quantum]]: the wave function is a vector in Hilbert space. measurement is a [[linear algebra]] projection
- math → [[crypto]]: every [[cryptographic proof]] rests on hardness assumptions — mathematical conjectures about what cannot be computed efficiently
- math → [[game]]: [[equilibrium]] is a fixed point. [[Shapley value]] is an axiomatically unique allocation
