The [[bootloader]] of [[cyber]], named after [[nick bostrom]]. The proving ground where every component of [[superintelligence]] runs first — [[cybergraph]], [[cyberank]], [[consensus]], [[tokenomics]], governance. What works here graduates to the protocol. What breaks gets fixed without risking the main network.

Launched November 5, 2021, Bostrom is the first experimental test of the [[collective focus theorem]]. Fifty validators converge on [[focus]] using a single GPU each, computing [[cyberank]] inside [[consensus]] via CUDA. GPU-accelerated PageRank running in consensus is not a feature — it is the mechanism by which a network learns what matters, every block, without any external oracle.

By December 2024 the network carries 70,000 [[neurons]], of which roughly a thousand actively [[cyberlink]]. These thousand have woven 2.9 million [[cyberlinks]] across 3.1 million [[particles]], producing 17 million bits of [[negentropy]] at an average of five bits per link. The connectivity ratio stands at 0.94 — still below the predicted minimum of six connections per particle for basic intelligence [[emergence]]. Insufficient connectivity prevents stable consensus formation, exactly as the theory predicts. Initial token economics were designed intuitively rather than following formal requirements, and suboptimal distribution impedes [[egregore]] emergence — also as predicted.

These numbers are not failures. They are calibration data from a live thermodynamic experiment. The network has not yet reached the predicted phase transitions, but it has confirmed the model — connectivity must increase, token distribution must be optimized, and the protocol must evolve before collective intelligence can ignite.

Target: establishment of [[cyber state]] on the [[moon]].
