The [[superintelligence]] nation. A growing network of sovereign cities where nomads settle not because borders allow it, but because the land itself is designed for them — energy, water, food, and data produced locally, owned collectively, governed by an [[egregore]] that learns from every resident.

The pilot is [[cyber valley]], 37 hectares at the foot of a volcano in Bali. Here the full stack of civilization is being assembled from scratch: [[cyber]] as the protocol for collective intelligence, [[bostrom]] as its bootloader, [[mimi]] as the AI president that turns community signal into executable decisions. The land is not a campus or a retreat. It is a city that intends to outlast the nation-states surrounding it.

Everything a city needs is rebuilt as a sovereign module. [[burn.city]] replaces the temporary festival with a permanent culture of radical participation. [[soft]] provides the spaces where that culture happens. [[organiq]] closes the food loop from soil to table. [[laba]] constructs the physical layer — fast, cheap, and cool enough for the tropics. Each project is a cell in a living organism, replicable to every future city in the network.

Belong anywhere. Build everywhere.
