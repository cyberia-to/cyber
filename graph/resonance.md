phenomenon where a system [[oscillation]] reaches maximum [[amplitude]] at specific driving [[frequency]]

every physical system has one or more natural frequencies determined by its structure

when driving frequency matches natural frequency, energy transfer is maximized

mechanical resonance: tuning fork, bridge vibration, wine glass shattering at its resonant pitch

electrical resonance: LC circuits tuned to select specific radio [[frequency]] bands

orbital resonance: gravitational coupling locks planetary or satellite orbits into integer ratios

chemical resonance: electron delocalization across multiple bonding structures (benzene ring)

acoustic resonance: musical instruments amplify specific harmonics through resonant cavities

social resonance: ideas, memes, and movements amplify when they match cultural receptivity

resonance bridges [[physics]], [[chemistry]], [[biology]], [[engineering]], and social dynamics
