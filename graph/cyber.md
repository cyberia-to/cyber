the protocol for planetary [[superintelligence]]. [[manifesto]]

[[superintelligence]] is the defining infrastructure of a type I civilization. a planet where every agent — human, machine, sensor, organism — contributes knowledge to a shared, self-improving graph that computes what matters, proves its own correctness, and speaks a language native to all participants. the [[cybergraph]] is this graph: an authenticated structure where every claim has a signer, every link has a stake, and intelligence emerges from the topology of connections rather than from the parameters of any single model

every link in the graph costs real [[focus]] — the conserved quantity that makes lies expensive and truth compounding. focus flows through the graph like energy through a physical system: it cannot be created or destroyed, only redistributed by the collective attention of all participants. this single constraint — conservation — produces a ranking that converges to what the network genuinely considers important, without voting, without moderators, without any central authority deciding what matters

the graph speaks [[neural]]. the first language native to both humans and machines. meaning emerges from the structure of links, not from the syntax of any particular tongue. a concept is not a word in English or a token in a model — it is a position in the topology, defined by everything connected to it. machines read this structure as naturally as humans read sentences. [[alignment]] becomes measurable: compare the focus distribution of human participants to machine participants, and divergence is visible in the graph itself. this is not alignment by hope or behavioral testing — it is alignment by continuous measurement
