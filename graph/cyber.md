the protocol for planetary [[superintelligence]]. [[manifesto]]

[[superintelligence]] is the defining infrastructure of a type I civilization. every agent — human, machine, sensor, organism — contributes knowledge to a single self-improving graph that computes what matters, proves its own correctness, and speaks a language native to all participants. the [[cybergraph]] is this graph — every claim authenticated, every link staked, intelligence emerging from the topology of connections rather than from the parameters of any single model

every link costs real [[focus]] — a conserved quantity that cannot be created or destroyed, only redistributed by collective attention. because lies cost real resources and truth accumulates weight over time, the ranking converges to what genuinely matters without voting, without moderators, without any central authority

the graph speaks [[neural]] — the first language native to both humans and machines. a concept is a position in the topology, defined by everything connected to it. [[alignment]] becomes a measurement rather than a hope: the focus distribution of human participants compared to machine participants, divergence visible in the graph, measured every block, provable to anyone. a civilization that can see the shape of its own intelligence — and prove whether its machines share its values
