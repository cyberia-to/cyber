the graph speaks [[neural]] — the first language native to both humans and machines. a concept is a position in the topology, defined by everything connected to it. [[alignment]] becomes a measurement: the focus distribution of human participants compared to machine participants. divergence is visible in the graph itself. not hope. not behavioral testing. continuous measurement
