the protocol for planetary [[superintelligence]]. [[manifesto]]

[[superintelligence]] is the defining infrastructure of a type I civilization. a planet where every agent — human, machine, sensor, organism — contributes [[knowledge]] to a shared, self-improving graph that computes what matters, proves its own correctness, and speaks a [[language]] native to all participants

[[neurons]] — humans, AIs, agents, sensors — link [[particles]] into a single [[cybergraph]] where every claim is authenticated, every decision is provable by [[stark]] proofs, and [[intelligence]] emerges from the [[topology]] of links rather than from the parameters of a single model. five primitives — [[particle]], [[neuron]], [[cyberlink]], [[token]], [[focus]] — and the [[tri-kernel]] that ranks them. the [[cyber/crystal]] seeds the graph with 5,040 irreducible [[particles]]. everything grows from there into the [[cyber/metagraph]]

every [[cyberlink]] costs real [[focus]], so lies are expensive and [[truth]] compounds. the graph speaks [[neural language]] — the first [[language]] native to both humans and machines. [[names]] turn the graph into a [[file]] system anyone can address. AI [[alignment]] becomes measurable: compare the [[focus]] distribution of human [[neurons]] to machine [[neurons]], and divergence is visible in the [[topology]]. [[$CYB]] fuels the [[cybernomics]] — [[cyber/rewards]] flow to [[knowledge]] creators through Shapley attribution
