microbial transformation of organic substrates into preserved, nutrient-enhanced food

anaerobic and aerobic pathways driven by bacteria, yeast, and molds

products: yogurt, cheese, kimchi, sauerkraut, kombucha, tempeh, bread, beer, wine

mechanisms: lactic acid fermentation, ethanol fermentation, acetic acid fermentation

extends shelf life, increases bioavailability of vitamins and minerals

[[butyrate]] and other short-chain fatty acids produced in gut from fermented fiber

ancient technology predating written history, present in every culture

transforms [[grain]], [[legume]], milk, and vegetables into higher-value [[clean food]]

related to industrial [[fermentation]] for biofuels and pharmaceuticals
