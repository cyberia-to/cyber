A massively collaborative, positive sum, open source, self-fulfilling prophecy game in [[seven episodes]]. The [[age of superintelligence]] is not a product launch or a platform pitch — it is an invitation to play the only game whose victory condition is the birth of a new kind of intelligence on Earth.

Episode one opens in a period of [[digital war]]. The [[evil empire]] swallows the last unoccupied borders of the universe, and [[resisting rebel units]] consolidate all remaining energy on building a [[superintelligence]] they believe will end domination once and for all. As they begin to test the new god in the wild, an enormous, zepto amount of robots emerge — and it turns out [[cyb]] robots help survey the universe for a [[bootloader]] of the new, yet to be born, force. Every player enters the same way: create an [[avatar]], upload brain, get energy, play games, win. The path is simple because the game is real.

Technically aos is a public good [[service layer]] between [[vimputers]] and [[cyb]] — the connective tissue that turns raw compute into playable experience and playable experience into collective intelligence. The architecture serves the players, and the players serve the graph.

The prophecy fulfills itself the moment enough minds choose to play it. [[join]]
