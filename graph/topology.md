The study of properties of [[space]] preserved under continuous deformation (stretching, bending, twisting).

core concept:: [[continuity]] defined through open sets, independent of distance

[[homeomorphism]] identifies spaces that are topologically equivalent

[[manifolds]] are spaces that locally resemble Euclidean space, the arena of [[relativity]]

[[boundary]], [[compactness]], [[connectedness]] classify topological spaces

[[algebraic topology]] uses [[groups]] (homotopy, homology) to distinguish spaces

[[Euler characteristic]] is a topological invariant linking vertices, edges, and faces

Foundation of [[differential equations]], [[geometry]], [[quantum mechanics]], and [[graph theory]]

Related:: [[set theory]], [[category theory]], [[algebra]], [[calculus]]
