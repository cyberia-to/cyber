inception is the block height at which a [[neuron]] declares a [[signal]] or [[intent]] — the moment the action is committed to the [[cybergraph]]

inception is the first timestamp of a two-part duration. together with [[sealing]], it defines the span of a [[signal]]:

```
span = sealing_height − inception_height
```

a [[signal]] declared but not yet sealed exists as an [[intent]] from inception height onward. the [[cybergraph]] records the inception when the [[identity proof]] is published, making the declaration permanent and observable before finalization

inception does not require computation to be complete — it is a commitment to act, not proof that the act succeeded. the proof arrives at [[sealing]]
