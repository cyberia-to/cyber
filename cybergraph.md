[[files]], linked by [[cyberlinks]] that [[neurons]] sign in [[cyber/signal|signals]] and [[tokens]] weigh. a cyberlink holds the [[particles]] — the names — of the files it links; the math of the graph runs on those names alone. five primitives: [[file|files]], [[cyberlink|cyberlinks]], [[neuron|neurons]], [[token|tokens]], [[focus]]

built for a mole of connections — the threshold where individual links become collective intelligence the way individual molecules become a life. every claim signed, every link staked, the whole structure proving its own correctness
