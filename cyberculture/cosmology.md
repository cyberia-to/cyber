# cosmology

The cosmology slot of a [[cyberculture]] is one sentence about what the world is, repeated by people who did not write it. It turns the metaphysics and cosmogony of [[cybersophy]] into a shared account of reality.

For [[cyber]], that sentence is: reality is computation rendering state. The [[cyberverse]] is its implementation; the [[cybergraph]] is its shared, inscribed memory.
