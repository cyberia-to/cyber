# aesthetics

The aesthetics slot of a [[cyberculture]] is a recognizable answer to what a beautiful [[cybergraph]] looks like. It gives members a shared judgment of [[cybics/meta/order|structure]], [[cybics/lang/name|naming]], [[density]], and visual [[form]].

For [[cyber]], [[cybics/socio/aesthetics|beauty]] is a graph whose provenance is visible, whose distinctions remain legible, and whose connections open paths to [[knowledge]]. The standard becomes cultural when independent [[neurons]] produce and recognize that form.
