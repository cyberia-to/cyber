# aesthetics

The aesthetics slot of a [[cyberculture]] is a recognizable answer to what a beautiful [[cybergraph]] looks like. It gives members a shared judgment of structure, naming, density, and visual form.

For [[cyber]], beauty is a graph whose provenance is visible, whose distinctions remain legible, and whose connections open paths to [[knowledge]]. The standard becomes cultural when independent [[neurons]] produce and recognize that form.
