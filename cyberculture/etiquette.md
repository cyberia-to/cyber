# etiquette

The etiquette slot of a [[cyberculture]] is a shared sense of a good [[cyberlink|link]] versus spam, enforced by disapproval. It is the social layer of attention: members learn which connections deserve to be repeated.

A good link adds context, provenance, or a useful path for another [[neuron]]. Spam extracts attention while adding no knowledge. Protocol cost, [[focus]], and [[rank]] support the norm; disapproval gives it cultural force.
