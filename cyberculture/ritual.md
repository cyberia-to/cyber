# ritual

The ritual slot of a [[cyberculture]] is a set of repeated acts that signal [[cyberia|membership]]: [[cybersophy|key ceremonies]], [[cybersophy|first inscriptions]], and the deliberate creation of a [[cyberlink]].

A ritual gives a [[neuron]] a repeatable way to enter, affirm, or renew participation. Its meaning comes from shared practice; the [[signature]] and [[russian school of inscription|inscription]] make the act legible to the [[cybergraph]].
