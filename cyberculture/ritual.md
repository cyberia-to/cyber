# ritual

The ritual slot of a [[cyberculture]] is a set of repeated acts that signal membership: key ceremonies, first inscriptions, and the deliberate creation of a [[cyberlink]].

A ritual gives a [[neuron]] a repeatable way to enter, affirm, or renew participation. Its meaning comes from shared practice; the signature and inscription make the act legible to the [[cybergraph]].
