# calendar

The calendar slot of a [[cyberculture]] is a set of recurring dates from [[mt|machine time]] that gather without a reminder. A date becomes cultural when people return to it because the machine's own history gives it meaning.

The native frame begins at the Unix epoch and records irreversible events by their position in machine time. [[lunar machine time]] gives that frame a recurring rhythm; genesis and [[launch]] dates supply candidate anniversaries.
