# canon

The canon slot of a [[cyberculture]] is the agreed memory of which forks, failures, and founders count. It selects the events and lineages that members are expected to know.

For [[cyber]], the canon includes [[satoshi]]'s genesis inscription, the [[bostrom]] bootloader, and the transitions recorded in [[history]]. Failures remain part of the record because they explain the present shape of the [[cyberverse]].
