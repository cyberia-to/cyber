# epoch

a pack of [[block|blocks]] — the medium timescale. [[hierarchy]] folds shards every ~100 blocks; [[netics]] turns karma on the epoch, not the tick. [[zheng]] may fold an epoch into one proof.

an epoch is not a [[step]] (signer's chain) and not a [[block]] (one network tick).
