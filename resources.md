[[$CYB|CYB]] is spent on the resources a [[neuron]] needs to think. the markets are peer-to-peer: no company in the middle, no invoice, a payment is a payment.

three primitives:

**compute** — prove, fold, run. a machine that can execute [[nox]] or a [[zheng]] prover sells cycles to a neuron that cannot. cycles only. not an answer.

**storage** — keep [[particle]]s available. pay to put a file on the graph, pay to retrieve it. the store is other neurons, not a bucket with a brand.

**bandwidth** — move the graph. gossip, sync, openings. pay for the pipe between neighborhoods.

the fourth is a compound, not a fourth primitive.

**[[inference]]** — you pay for an answer. it burns compute, storage and bandwidth, and it also burns the local [[knowledge]] of the neuron that infers. the asking agents' opinion is in the query: who asks, what they already believe, what they want ranked. that is why inference is not a slice of compute. a rented model sells cycles with a prompt bolted on. here the answer is a derivative of all three markets plus the inferrer's graph — and it is addressed to the asker.

all four settle in CYB. mint is [[mining]] and [[staking]]; spend is here.
